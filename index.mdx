Totalis turns individual prediction market bets into custom parlays. Pick 2 to 5 outcomes across
Kalshi, Polymarket, and other venues, and combine them into one leveraged position. Market makers
compete to price it, and trades settle on Solana.

## Try it without an account

Market data is public. This call needs no key and no signup:

```bash
curl "https://api.totalis.trade/markets?limit=3"
```

Everything else needs a key. [Quickstart](/guides/quickstart) gets you to an authenticated call in
about five minutes.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Build a parlay">
    Pick 2 to 5 outcomes from any supported market and set your bet amount.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get competitive quotes">
    Open a quote request. Market makers compete in real time to offer the best payout odds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Trade and settle">
    Accept a quote. Your stake and the maker's collateral lock into on-chain Solana vaults, and the
    position settles automatically once the markets resolve.
  </Step>
</Steps>
