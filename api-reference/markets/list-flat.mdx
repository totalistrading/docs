---
title: 'List markets (flat)'
description: 'A flat, paginated feed of individual markets, ungrouped. Built for market makers and partners syncing the tradeable universe.'
openapi: 'GET /v1/markets/list'
---

<Note>
  **Building parlays.** Every market carries an `exclusion_keys` array. Two
  markets can share a parlay only if their arrays do not intersect. A plain
  set-intersection check, with no correlation rules to maintain client-side.
</Note>

All prices (`yes_bid`, `yes_ask`, `no_bid`, `no_ask`, `last_price`) are probabilities as decimals in `[0, 1]`, regardless of venue. Kalshi's cent prices are normalized.

By default the feed returns tradeable markets only (`status` of `active` or `open`); pass `status=all` for every status. Paging stops once `offset + limit` passes 10,000, so a full sync splits the universe with filters such as `venue` or `category` rather than paging one long list.

<Tip>
  For every market in a single game, `GET /markets/events/{event_ticker}`
  returns one event's complete market set in a single request.
</Tip>
