---
title: 'List markets (flat)'
description: 'A flat, paginated feed of individual markets, ungrouped. Built for market makers and partners syncing the tradeable universe.'
openapi: 'GET /v1/markets/list'
---

<Note>
  **Building parlays.** Every market carries an `exclusion_keys` array. Two
  markets can share a parlay only if their arrays do not intersect. A plain
  set-intersection check is all you need, with no correlation rules to
  maintain client-side.
</Note>

All prices (`yes_bid`, `yes_ask`, `no_bid`, `no_ask`, `last_price`) are decimal probabilities in `[0, 1]` on every venue. Kalshi's cent prices are normalized.

The feed returns tradeable markets only (`status` of `active` or `open`) by default. Pass `status=all` for every status.

Paging stops once `offset + limit` passes 10,000. For a full sync, split the universe with filters such as `venue` or `category` instead of paging one long list.

<Tip>
  `GET /markets/events/{event_ticker}` returns every market in one event,
  such as a single game, in one request.
</Tip>
