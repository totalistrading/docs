---
title: 'Deposit into the vault'
description: 'Move USDC from your wallet into your on-chain vault ahead of trading. Optional for bettors, usual for market makers.'
openapi: 'POST /v1/vault/deposit'
---

<Note>
You do not need this call to trade. Placing a parlay moves the stake from your wallet into the vault
in the same step. Use it to hold collateral in the vault ahead of time, which market makers do
before quoting. The vault is created on your first trade, so this call returns `404` until then.
Needs the `funds:deposit` scope and trading enabled. Limited to 10 calls per minute.
</Note>
