---
title: 'Deposit into the vault'
description: 'Move USDC from your wallet into your on-chain vault. Optional: trading and quoting both draw on wallet USDC too.'
openapi: 'POST /v1/vault/deposit'
---

<Note>
You do not need this call to trade or to quote. Placing a parlay moves the stake from your wallet into
the vault in the same step, and maker quotes are backed by wallet USDC plus vault free balance. Your
vault is created with your first filled position, as bettor or maker, so this call returns `404`
until then. Needs the `funds:deposit` scope and trading enabled. Limited to 10 calls per minute per
account.
</Note>
