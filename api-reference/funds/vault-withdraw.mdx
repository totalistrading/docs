---
title: 'Withdraw from the vault'
description: 'Move free balance from your on-chain vault back to your wallet. To send USDC out of Totalis, use the wallet withdraw instead.'
openapi: 'POST /v1/vault/withdraw'
---

<Note>
This moves USDC from the vault to your wallet only. Only the free balance moves; the on-chain
program enforces that locked collateral stays put. To send USDC to an external address, use
[`POST /v1/wallet/withdraw`](/api-reference/funds/wallet-withdraw), which taps the vault for you.
Needs the `funds:withdraw` scope. Limited to 10 calls per minute.
</Note>
