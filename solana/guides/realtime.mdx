Totalis has four data channels. Pick one by the phase of the trade you are in and the delivery
guarantee you need.

<img className="hidden dark:block rounded-2xl" src="/images/pictures/realtime-and-data-dark.png" alt="Which channel covers which stretch of a trade: quote stream before commit, WebSocket live after, webhooks from settlement, REST any time" />

| Channel | What it carries | Use it for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [SSE streams](/solana/api-reference/quote-service/stream) | Pre-trade pricing. Live `best_quote` updates on a quote request or cashout request. | Watching prices while you decide whether to commit |
| [WebSocket](/solana/guides/websocket) | Post-trade events, live. Quote acceptance, confirmation, position created and settled. | Live UIs and bots that hold a connection |
| [Webhooks](/solana/guides/webhooks) | Post-trade events, durable. The same lifecycle moments as HMAC signed POSTs, retried, then dead-lettered and replayable. | Server systems of record, with failed deliveries monitored and replayed |
| [REST reads](/solana/guides/reading-data) | State on demand. Portfolio, parlays, settlement detail, P&L, balances. | Backfill, reconciliation, on-demand display |

REST, SSE and the WebSocket authenticate with your [API key](/solana/authentication). Webhooks are signed with your endpoint's secret instead: [verify each delivery](/solana/guides/webhooks#verifying-signatures).

## One trade, three phases

**Pricing is SSE.** Each quote request has its own stream at
[`GET /v1/quote-requests/{id}/stream`](/solana/api-reference/quote-service/stream). Each cashout request
has one at [`GET /v1/cashout-requests/{id}/stream`](/solana/api-reference/quote-service/cashout-stream). A
stream exists only while its request is live and closes after a terminal event. Market makers watch
the firehose variant, [`GET /v1/mm/quote-requests/stream`](/solana/api-reference/quote-service-mm/stream).

**From commit onward it is events.** When you commit, the quote request becomes an
[RFQ](/solana/glossary#the-noun-stack) and the SSE stream ends. Acceptance, confirmation, on-chain
execution, and settlement arrive on the [WebSocket](/solana/guides/websocket) and as
[webhooks](/solana/guides/webhooks).

**State is always readable.** The [read endpoints](/solana/guides/reading-data) return current truth
whenever you ask. Use them to backfill after downtime and to reconcile what the push channels told
you.

## WebSocket or webhooks

Both deliver post-trade lifecycle events and overlap for most of the lifecycle. They differ in the
delivery guarantee.

| | WebSocket | Webhooks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Delivery | To a connected client, in real time | POST to your server, at-least-once |
| Missed while disconnected | Not redelivered | Retried with backoff, then dead-lettered |
| Signed | No, the connection is authenticated | Yes, HMAC per delivery |
| Replay | No | [Yes](/solana/guides/webhooks#replaying-deliveries) |
| Best for | Live UIs and trading bots, where latency matters | Accounting, notifications, any system of record |

## Typical integrations

### Trading bot

1. Create a quote request with [`POST /v1/quote-requests`](/solana/api-reference/quote-service/create).
2. Watch its [SSE stream](/solana/api-reference/quote-service/stream) for `best_quote` events. Store
   `book_seq` and the quote `id`.
3. [Commit](/solana/api-reference/quote-service/commit) when the price is right.
4. Follow the trade live on the WebSocket
   [`rfq:{rfq_id}` channel](/solana/guides/websocket#subscribing-to-channels), or durably via the
   [`parlay.status_changed`](/solana/guides/webhooks#event-catalog) webhook.
5. Record the outcome from the `position.settled` webhook, or read it from
   [`GET /v1/rfqs/{id}`](/solana/api-reference/parlays/get).

### Read-only dashboard

1. Pull state with [`GET /v1/portfolio`](/solana/api-reference/funds/portfolio),
   [`GET /v1/rfqs`](/solana/api-reference/parlays/list), and [`GET /v1/pnl`](/solana/api-reference/funds/pnl).
2. Subscribe to [`rfq:{rfq_id}`](/solana/guides/websocket#subscribing-to-channels) for live position
   updates.
3. On reconnect, [reconcile](/solana/guides/reading-data#reconciling) against the read endpoints.

### Market maker

The maker loop, including cashout pricing, is on [Market making](/solana/guides/market-making).
