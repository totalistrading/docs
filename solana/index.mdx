Totalis turns individual prediction market bets into custom parlays. A **parlay** is one bet that
pays out only if every outcome in it happens. Pick outcomes on Kalshi or Polymarket and combine
them into one position. Market makers compete to price it,
and trades settle on Solana. Totalis is not available to U.S. persons.

## Try it without an account

Market data is public. This call needs no key and no signup:

```bash
curl "https://api.totalis.trade/markets?limit=3"
```

Everything else needs authentication: an [API key](/solana/authentication), or the app's Privy session. Start with the [Quickstart](/solana/quickstart).

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Build a parlay">
    Pick outcomes from any supported market and set your bet amount.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get competitive quotes">
    Open a quote request: your outcomes and amount, sent to market makers for pricing. They compete
    in real time to offer the best payout odds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Trade and settle">
    Accept a quote. When the maker confirms, your stake and the maker's collateral lock in on-chain
    Solana vaults. The position settles automatically when the markets resolve.
  </Step>
</Steps>
