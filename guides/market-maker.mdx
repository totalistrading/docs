Market makers stream incoming parlay quote requests over SSE, submit competitive prices, and earn
the spread when users commit and confirm trades.

There is no separate market maker role. Any authenticated user with an API key that holds the
`mm:quote` scope can quote.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect to the quote request stream">
    Open an SSE connection to
    [`GET /v1/mm/quote-requests/stream`](/api-reference/quote-service-mm/stream) to receive live
    quote requests. On connect you get a snapshot of all active requests, then incremental updates.

    Match on the exact event names in the
    [event summary table](/api-reference/quote-service-mm/stream#sse-event-types). The separators on
    this stream are not consistent: `quote_request` and `quote_request_expired` use underscores,
    while `quote_request:updated` uses a colon.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit draft quotes">
    Analyze the legs and submit payout odds via
    [`PUT /v1/mm/quote-requests/{id}/quote`](/api-reference/quote-service-mm/submit-quote) for the
    exact `version` and `request_hash` you received.

    <Warning>
    Price `payout_odds` and size collateral against the net stake (`user_stake`), never against
    `bet_amount`. The taker fee comes off first, so `user_stake` is what your odds are applied to.
    Quoting off the gross bet overprices every quote you submit. See
    [Limits and fees](/guides/limits-and-fees#fees).
    </Warning>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm trades">
    When a user commits your quote, Totalis creates an accepted RFQ and sends `quote:accepted` on
    `mm:quotes:{mm_id}`. The same subscription also receives `mm_quote:accepted`, which carries
    details visible only to you: `user_id`, final quote economics, and exposure legs.

    Confirm with [`POST /v1/mm/quotes/{quoteId}/confirm`](/api-reference/mm/confirm-quote) before
    `confirmation_deadline`. Honor the timestamp on the event rather than assuming the default
    window. The user's stake and your collateral then lock together in on-chain Solana vaults.

    After a dropped connection or a restart, reconcile with
    [`GET /v1/mm/quotes?status=accepted`](/api-reference/mm/list-quotes) and confirm anything still
    open.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Core quoting loop

| Step | Endpoint |
| --- | --- |
| Pull market context and current prices | [`GET /markets`](/api-reference/markets/list) |
| Check invalid leg combinations | `exclusion_keys` on [`GET /v1/markets/list`](/api-reference/markets/list-flat) |
| Stream live quote requests | [`GET /v1/mm/quote-requests/stream`](/api-reference/quote-service-mm/stream) |
| Submit a draft quote | [`PUT /v1/mm/quote-requests/{id}/quote`](/api-reference/quote-service-mm/submit-quote) |
| Withdraw a draft quote | [`DELETE /v1/mm/quote-requests/{id}/quote`](/api-reference/quote-service-mm/withdraw-quote) |
| Receive committed trades | `quote:accepted` on `mm:quotes:{mm_id}` |
| Receive accepted trade details | `mm_quote:accepted` on `mm:quotes:{mm_id}` |
| Confirm an accepted quote | [`POST /v1/mm/quotes/{quoteId}/confirm`](/api-reference/mm/confirm-quote) |
| Reconcile quotes after a disconnect | [`GET /v1/mm/quotes`](/api-reference/mm/list-quotes) |

Two markets can share a parlay only if their `exclusion_keys` arrays do not intersect.

Check available USDC and locked funds with [`GET /v1/wallet`](/api-reference/user/get-wallet) before
quoting at size.

## Quoting rules

- One active quote per request version. Submitting again replaces your previous quote.
- Send `request_version`, `request_hash`, `payout_odds` and `expires_in_ms`, nothing else. The server
  derives the money fields from your odds and the net stake: `user_cost = bet_amount - taker_fee`,
  `total_payout = user_cost * payout_odds`, `mm_cost = total_payout - user_cost`.
- Keep quotes short-lived. The default validity is 15 seconds.
- When the request moves to a new version, your quote on the old version is invalidated for you.
  Re-price and `PUT` again with the new version and hash. There is nothing to withdraw first.
- To stand down on the current version before the user commits, `DELETE` the quote. That is the only
  reason to withdraw.

## Your identity across channels

Your `mm_id` is your `user_id`, which is your Privy DID (`did:privy:...`). It is echoed by
`auth:success` on the WebSocket and by the
[`connected`](/api-reference/quote-service-mm/stream#connected) event on the MM SSE stream, and it
is the key in the `mm:quotes:{mm_id}` channel name.

<Warning>
`counterparty_mm_id` is not your id and not the bidder's id. It names the maker who already backs a
position. On a broadcast cashout auction that is usually not the maker whose bid wins. See the
[Glossary](/guides/glossary#identifiers).
</Warning>

## Pricing early cashouts

A user can leave an active position before it settles. Totalis opens a short auction on that
position and broadcasts it to every market maker, on the stream you already consume, as a
[`cashout_request`](/api-reference/quote-service-mm/stream#cashout_request) event.

You reply with one all-in price via
[`PUT /v1/mm/cashout-requests/{id}/quote`](/api-reference/quote-service-mm/cashout-quote). The
highest live bid wins and ties go to the earliest bid. There is no decline endpoint. To pass, submit
nothing.

`price_micro` is what the user receives, all-in, in microUSDC. It may be below their stake. What a
win costs you depends on whether you already back that position: a closing exit carries the profit
fee on your side, while acquiring a position does not.

See [Cashout auctions](/guides/cashout-auctions) for the auction rules, the payload, and the error
reasons.

## Collateral minimization

Without portfolio-level netting, each parlay would post collateral in isolation. A parlay with a
12,000 USDC max payout would need 12,000 USDC in the vault, an 8,000 USDC max payout parlay would
need another 8,000, and so on.

That is wasteful when positions are mutually exclusive and cannot both come true. The real worst
case is one parlay hitting, not both.

<img className="block dark:hidden rounded-2xl" src="/images/pictures/collateral-netting-light.png" alt="Two parlays that cannot both win: posted in isolation they lock 20,000, margined as a portfolio they lock 12,000" />
<img className="hidden dark:block rounded-2xl" src="/images/pictures/collateral-netting-dark.png" alt="Two parlays that cannot both win: posted in isolation they lock 20,000, margined as a portfolio they lock 12,000" />

Totalis margins market makers at the portfolio level instead. It computes the true worst case loss
across all possible outcome states for the whole book, and requires collateral only for that
scenario.

Modeled across 3 to 5 leg parlays spanning 1000 markets, this cuts required collateral by 30% to 40%
compared with posting each trade in isolation.
