---
title: 'Funding your wallet'
description: 'Send USDC to your Solana wallet, enable trading, and withdraw. There is no separate deposit call.'
---

You fund your account by sending USDC to your Solana wallet address. Once it lands you are ready to
trade. Placing a parlay pulls your stake into the vault automatically, so there is no separate
deposit step.

## Enable trading first

Before you can place a parlay or withdraw, your wallet must be delegated. You grant Totalis
permission to sign transactions on your behalf inside a secure enclave. See
[Vaults and custody](/guides/vault-architecture) for what that permission does and does not cover.

<Warning>
Enabling trading is an interactive step. You approve a consent dialog in the app, once. It cannot be
done from the REST API. `POST /v1/wallet/enable-trading` only reports status. It does not grant the
permission.
</Warning>

Check the current state with
[`GET /v1/wallet/delegation-status`](/api-reference/funds/delegation-status).

## Send USDC to your wallet

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get your wallet address">
    [`GET /v1/wallet`](/api-reference/user/get-wallet) returns your Solana wallet `address` and
    current balances.

    ```bash
    curl https://api.totalis.trade/v1/wallet \
      -H "X-API-Key: $TOTALIS_API_KEY"
    ```

    The same address is under **Deposit** in the app, as a QR code and a copy button. The dialog
    watches the chain and updates as soon as USDC lands.

    <Frame caption="Deposit in the app: the wallet address, the chain warning, and the card and bridge options">
      <img src="/images/app/01-deposit-chooser.png" alt="The Deposit dialog showing a USDC on Solana address with a QR code, a warning to send only USDC on Solana, and options to pay by card or bridge from another chain" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Send native USDC on Solana">
    Transfer USDC to that address from any wallet or exchange that supports SPL tokens. The chain,
    token, and mint are on [Limits and fees](/guides/limits-and-fees#network).

    <Warning>
    Send only native Solana USDC at the documented mint. Bridged USDC, other tokens, and transfers
    on another chain are not credited and may be unrecoverable.
    </Warning>

    You do not need SOL. Network fees are sponsored, so your wallet only ever holds USDC.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Confirm it landed">
    Poll [`GET /v1/wallet`](/api-reference/user/get-wallet) until the balance reflects your
    transfer. You are now ready to request quotes and place parlays.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How your balance is used

There is no deposit call because the vault funds itself at trade time.

| Where the money is | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Wallet | USDC you send lands here. |
| Vault | When you place a parlay, your stake moves from the wallet into the vault and locks, in one step. |
| Eligibility | Checked against your combined wallet and vault free balance. If the total covers your stake, the trade goes through. |

Field by field definitions of each balance are in the
[Glossary](/guides/glossary#balance-fields).

## Withdrawing

Use [`POST /v1/wallet/withdraw`](/api-reference/funds/wallet-withdraw) with a recipient and amount.

```bash
curl -X POST https://api.totalis.trade/v1/wallet/withdraw \
  -H "X-API-Key: $TOTALIS_API_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{ "recipient": "<solana-address>", "amount_usdc": 50 }'
```

If the amount exceeds your wallet balance, Totalis draws the remainder from your vault's free
balance, which is settled winnings and unlocked collateral. One call therefore reaches everything
that is not locked in an open position.

Withdrawing requires trading to be enabled, the same delegation as placing a parlay. It also
requires the `funds:withdraw` scope, which is the highest risk scope on a key. See
[Authentication](/guides/authentication#scopes).
