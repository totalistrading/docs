A parlay starts as a **quote request**: your legs, priced live by market makers. It becomes a real
**position** the moment you commit to a quote. From there it settles automatically when the
underlying markets resolve, or you close it early.

## At a glance

- A parlay is 2 to 5 legs on one bet of 1 to 100 USDC, priced live by competing market makers.
- Your funds move once, when the maker confirms your trade. Nothing is touched before that.
- The position then goes live on chain and settles by itself when the last market resolves.
- A live position can be closed early through a short cashout auction, about 10 seconds.

Collateral locks at `confirmed`. Before that point no funds have moved, and a parlay that ends at
`cancelled` or `expired` never touches your balance.

## Place a parlay

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a quote request">
    Add 2 to 5 legs and a bet amount. Each leg names a market and a side (`yes` or `no`). The
    `market_ticker` is venue specific:

    - **Kalshi**: the market ticker, for example `KXBTC-25FEB07-T100000`.
    - **Polymarket**: the `condition_id`, the `0x` prefixed hex string. Not the slug and not the
      question id. Both sides of a binary share one `condition_id` and are told apart by `side`.

    <Accordion title="The call">
    ```bash
    POST /v1/quote-requests
    {
      "legs": [
        { "market_ticker": "KXBTC-25FEB07-T100000", "side": "yes", "venue": "kalshi" },
        { "market_ticker": "0x4d2...",              "side": "no",  "venue": "polymarket" }
      ],
      "bet_amount": 25
    }
    ```
    </Accordion>

    Totalis validates each market, applies leg exclusion rules, and broadcasts the request to market
    makers. Correlated legs cannot be combined. See
    [Create quote request](/api-reference/quote-service/create) for the full field reference and
    [Limits and fees](/guides/limits-and-fees) for the constraints.

    <Warning>
    The amount that locks is your bet amount minus a 1% taker fee, charged upfront. A 25 USDC bet
    locks a 24.75 net stake. Do not expect the locked stake to match `bet_amount`.
    </Warning>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Market makers quote it">
    Makers stream the request and submit quotes with payout odds. You watch them arrive on the quote
    request [stream](/api-reference/quote-service/stream). The best one is always on top.
    <Accordion title="What a quote carries">
    | Field | Meaning |
    | --- | --- |
    | `user_cost` | Your net stake, `bet_amount` minus the taker fee. |
    | `payout_odds` | The multiple applied to your net stake, never to the gross bet. |
    | `total_payout` | `user_cost * payout_odds`. What you receive if every leg wins. |
    | `book_seq` | The SSE event's sequence number. You send it back at commit to prove your pricing was current. |
    </Accordion>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Commit the best quote">
    Accept a quote to lock the trade. Totalis selects the best still valid quote for the version you
    saw, and creates the parlay with that quote already accepted.

    <Accordion title="The call">
    ```bash
    POST /v1/quote-requests/<id>/commit
    {
      "expected_version": 3,
      "displayed_quote_id": "<quote-id>",
      "displayed_quote_book_seq": 5,
      "min_payout_odds_seen": 4.0
    }
    ```
    </Accordion>
    The response returns the real `rfq_id`, the accepted quote, and the payout odds. Status becomes
    `accepted`. If the commit fails, no funds moved and no trade happened. See
    [Errors](/api-reference/errors) for the rejection reasons.
  </Step>

  <Step title="The parlay funds on-chain">
    The maker confirms, and your stake and the maker's collateral lock atomically in
    [on-chain Solana vaults](/guides/vault-architecture). Gas is sponsored, so you only ever need
    USDC.

    Status moves to `confirmed`, then `executed` once the position is live on-chain. If the maker
    does not confirm in time, the parlay reopens for other makers, or expires if its window has
    passed. Your funds are never touched in that path.
  </Step>

  <Step title="It settles automatically">
    When every leg's market has resolved, the position settles:

    - **Every leg wins.** You receive the full payout, your stake plus the maker's collateral, minus
      the profit fee.
    - **Any leg loses.** Your stake transfers to the maker, minus the fee on their profit.
    - **A leg is voided.** The position is cancelled, both sides' collateral is released, and nobody
      wins or loses.

    Status becomes `settled`. Read the outcome and transaction signatures from
    [`GET /v1/rfqs/{id}`](/api-reference/parlays/get).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Closing early

Once a parlay is live on chain you can leave it before settlement. Totalis runs a short auction among
market makers, you see the best all-in price live, and you accept it or not. Nothing is ever filled
at a price you did not see. [Early cashout](/guides/early-cashout) has the steps and the two ways an
auction can resolve.

## Status reference

The `status` on a parlay. In the API the parlay object is called an RFQ, so the field lives on `GET /v1/rfqs/{id}`:

| Status | Meaning | Funds |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `open` | Awaiting quotes, or reopened after a maker missed confirmation. | none moved |
| `quoted` | At least one maker has quoted. | none moved |
| `accepted` | You committed a quote. Awaiting maker confirmation. | none moved |
| `confirmed` | Maker confirmed. Funding on-chain. | locking |
| `executed` | Live on-chain. Eligible for early cashout. | locked |
| `settled` | Resolved and paid out. | released |
| `bought_back` | Closed early via a cashout that the backing maker won. | released |
| `cancelled` | Ended before funding, or a leg was voided. | released or never moved |
| `expired` | Ended before funding. | none moved |

Quotes carry their own status: `pending` while live, `accepted`, `confirmed` and `executed` as the
trade progresses, `withdrawn` when the maker pulled it, `expired` when its validity lapsed, and
`rejected` when you accepted a different quote on the same request.

## Constraints

Leg counts, bet range, odds range, and every timing window live on
