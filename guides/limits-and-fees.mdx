The limits, windows, rate limits and fees that shape an integration are on this page. Endpoint pages
repeat the caps that apply to them.

## At a glance

- 2 to 5 legs, 1 to 100 USDC per bet, payout odds between 1.0001 and 1000.
- Two fees, both 1%: a taker fee on the bet, charged upfront, and a profit fee on the winner's profit at settlement. A 25 USDC bet at 4.0 odds pays 98.26 if every leg wins.
- Odds apply to the net stake after the taker fee, never to the gross bet.
- Every expiring object carries its own timestamp. Honor it over any default on this page.

## Parlay construction

| Constraint | Value | Enforced at |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Legs per parlay | 2 to 5 | [Create quote request](/api-reference/quote-service/create) |
| Bet amount | 1 to 100 USDC | Create quote request |
| Payout odds | 1.0001 to 1000 | [Submit quote](/api-reference/quote-service-mm/submit-quote) |
| Leg combinations | Two markets can share a parlay only if their `exclusion_keys` arrays do not intersect | Create quote request |

Correlated legs are rejected. Read each market's `exclusion_keys` from
[List markets](/api-reference/markets/list-flat) and intersect them yourself before you build a
request, rather than discovering the rejection at create time.

## Timing

| Window | Default | Range | What it governs |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Quote validity | 15 s | 5 to 60 s | How long a maker's submitted quote stays live (`expires_in_ms`) |
| Quote request expiry | 90 s | set by the server | How long your request accepts quotes. Each update restarts it. There is no request field for it |
| Maker confirmation | 5 s | 5 to 8 s | How long the winning maker has to confirm after you commit. Your commit call waits for it |
| Cashout auction | about 10 s | fixed | How long an early exit auction accepts bids |
| Cashout cutoff | 30 s | configurable | No cashout opens, and no maker quote is accepted, within this long of a position's or market's end |
| Cashout bid validity | 15000 ms | 5000 to 60000 ms | How long your cashout bid stays live |
| WebSocket authentication | 10 s | fixed | How long you have to send the auth message after connecting |

<Warning>
Do not hardcode these defaults. Every object that expires carries its own `expires_at` or
`valid_until` timestamp. Honor the timestamp on the object, not the number in this table. The
defaults are configurable, so the table tells you the shape of the window, not a guarantee for a
specific request.
</Warning>

If the maker misses the confirmation window, the parlay expires with reason
`mm_confirmation_timeout` and the hold on your bet is released. Your funds never move in that path.
Request quotes again to retry.

## Rate limits

There are three buckets. Which one a route debits depends on whether the limiter runs before or
after authentication, and on which service answers.

| Bucket | Routes | Keyed by | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Authenticated | `/v1/*` apart from the market and quote service routes below, plus `/app/*`, `/rfqs*`, `/parlay-shares*`, and the flat `/mm/quotes*` and `/mm/positions*` aliases | your account | 300 / minute, 100 without a key |
| Public | `/markets*`, `/v1/markets*`, `/referral*` | source IP | 100 / minute |
| Quote service | `/v1/quote-requests*`, `/v1/cashout-requests*`, `/v1/mm/quote-requests*`, `/v1/mm/cashout-requests*` | source IP | 300 / minute by default |

The first bucket is shared: every authenticated route on the main API draws down the same 300,
including the maker reads at `/v1/mm/quotes` and `/v1/mm/positions` and their flat `/mm/...`
aliases. The quote service routes are proxied to a separate service with its own per-IP limiter and
do not draw down the 300.

A few calls that move money carry their own cap as well, keyed to your account and counted on top
of the shared 300:

| Call | Limit |
| --- | --- |
| [`POST /v1/wallet/withdraw`](/api-reference/funds/wallet-withdraw) | 5 / minute |
| [`POST /v1/vault/deposit`](/api-reference/funds/vault-deposit), [`POST /v1/vault/withdraw`](/api-reference/funds/vault-withdraw) | 10 / minute each |
| [`POST /v1/wallet/submit-tx`](/api-reference/user/submit-tx) | 10 / minute |

What this means when you size a client:

- Market reads do **not** debit your authenticated budget.
- Sending an API key to a public read does **not** raise its limit. Those routes never resolve the
  key, so you stay on the per-IP bucket.
- Both per-IP buckets are per **source IP**, not per key. A market maker fleet behind one NAT shares
  a single 100 / minute market-read budget and a single quote service budget across every process
  and every key it runs.

Every response carries `X-RateLimit-Limit`, `X-RateLimit-Remaining`, and `X-RateLimit-Reset`. A
`429` response includes `error.retry_after`, in seconds. See [Errors](/api-reference/errors).

<Note>
`X-RateLimit-Limit` reports the ceiling that applied, not which counter produced it, so two routes
can both report `100` from different buckets. Watch `X-RateLimit-Remaining` to tell them apart.
</Note>

## Fees

Totalis charges two fees. They are charged at different times, against different bases.

| Fee | Rate | Base | When |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Taker fee | 1% | Your gross `bet_amount` | Upfront, when you place the trade |
| Profit fee | 1% | The winning side's profit | At settlement, or on a closing cashout |

The taker fee is why odds never apply to your gross bet. It comes off first, and everything
downstream is priced against what remains.

```
taker_fee    = floor(bet_amount_micro * 100 / 10000)   # 1% = 100 bps, integer microUSDC
user_stake   = bet_amount - taker_fee                  # called user_cost before commit
total_payout = user_stake * payout_odds
mm_risk      = total_payout - user_stake               # called mm_cost before commit
```

Worked example, matching the diagram. You bet 25 USDC and a maker quotes 4.0 payout odds:

| Step | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Bet amount | 25.00 |
| Taker fee (1%, upfront) | 0.25 |
| Net stake (`user_stake`) | 24.75 |
| Total payout at 4.0 odds | 99.00 |
| Maker's locked risk (`mm_risk`) | 74.25 |
| Your profit if every leg wins | 74.25 |
| Profit fee (1% of profit) | 0.7425 |
| You receive | 98.2575 |

<Note>
The fee rate is written onto the position when it is created. A later rate change never reprices a
position that is already live. Settlement always uses the rate that applied at trade time.
</Note>

### Fees on an early exit

An [early cashout](/guides/early-cashout) is a real settlement, not a refund. The bid you accept is
a single all-in figure with the profit fee already inside it. There is no separate fee line to
subtract, and no second taker fee for leaving.

### Referral share

A share of the trading fees generated by traders you refer is paid back to you in USDC. The rate,
the claim minimum, and how accrual works are on [Referrals](/referrals).
