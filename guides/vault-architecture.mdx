Totalis is non-custodial. Your funds sit in an on-chain Solana vault that the protocol's program
controls, not in a Totalis held account. You enable trading once by delegating signing permission.
After that, placing, settling, and closing parlays happens without you signing each transaction by
hand.

## Your vault

You have one persistent vault, reused across every parlay. It tracks three balances:

| Balance | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Gross | Total USDC in the vault. |
| Locked | Collateral committed to your active positions. |
| Free | `gross - locked`. Available to trade or withdraw. |

Read them any time from [`GET /v1/vault`](/api-reference/funds/get-vault). You fund the vault by
sending USDC to your wallet (see [Funding](/guides/funding)). Placing a parlay moves your stake into
the vault and locks it in one atomic step.

## Delegated signing

Your wallet is a Privy embedded wallet whose private key lives inside a Trusted Execution
Environment. The key never leaves the enclave, and Privy cannot extract it either.

To trade, you grant Totalis permission to sign on your behalf inside that enclave. This is a one
time consent in the app, described under [Enable trading](/guides/funding#enable-trading-first).

This is semi-custodial. You explicitly grant the permission and can revoke it at any time. Totalis
can sign your trades. It never holds your key and cannot move it.

## Gas is sponsored

Totalis pays the network fees for trading and settlement. Your wallet only ever needs to hold USDC,
never SOL.

## How a position locks and settles

When you commit to a quote, your stake and the maker's collateral lock together in a single atomic
transaction. There is no window where one side is funded and the other is not.

Settlement moves funds in exactly one of three ways:

| Outcome | Result |
| --- | --- |
| You win | You receive the maker's collateral, minus the profit fee on your profit. |
| You lose | Your stake transfers to the maker, minus the fee on their profit. |
| A leg is voided | The position is cancelled and both sides' collateral is released. Nobody wins or loses. |

The fee rate is written onto each position when it is created, so settlement always uses the rate
that applied at trade time. Both fees and the full math are on
[Limits and fees](/guides/limits-and-fees).

## What Totalis can and cannot do

A precise statement of the trust model, since "non-custodial" gets used loosely:

| Totalis can | Totalis cannot |
| --- | --- |
| Sign trading transactions inside the enclave, while your delegation is active | Extract or hold your private key |
| Create and settle positions against verified market outcomes | Move locked collateral to an arbitrary address |
| Collect the protocol fee defined on the position | Change the fee rate on a position that is already live |
| Cancel a position when a leg is voided | Prevent you from revoking delegation |

Every vault, position, and settlement is on the Solana blockchain and independently verifiable.

## Capital efficiency for makers

Market makers do not lock the full risk of every position independently. Totalis margins a maker's
book as a portfolio, so collateral reflects their incremental worst case exposure. Hedged or
offsetting positions require less locked capital than the naive sum. This is what lets makers quote
deep, competitive odds.
