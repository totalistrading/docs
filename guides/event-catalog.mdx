Each row below is one moment in a trade. Each column is one channel. Field names, identifiers, and
their aliases are in the [Glossary](/guides/glossary), along with the
[event naming rules](/guides/glossary#event-naming) for each channel.

<Note>
`quote:*` WebSocket events (`quote:accepted`, `quote:confirmed`, `quote:executed`, `quote:expired`,
`quote:rejected`, `quote:withdrawn`) go to the market maker whose quote they concern, on their private
`mm:quotes:{mm_id}` channel. The bettor's own authenticated connections also receive
`quote:confirmed` and `quote:executed` for their trade. `rfq:*` and `position:*` events go to `rfq:{rfq_id}`, the channel a
user or anyone tracking a specific trade subscribes to. `vault:deposited` and `vault:withdrawn` go to
the account's own connections and are not in the table.

The two families are not interchangeable. Some rows have a WebSocket event for the maker and none
for the user, or the reverse.
</Note>

## The acquisition gap

An early cashout resolves one of two ways. Only one of them notifies you live.

### A closing exit is well covered

`position:bought_back` carries `rfq_id`, `position_pda`, `amount`, `mm_pays_user`, `tx_signature`,
and `quote_id`. It fans out to four places at once:

| Destination | Needs a subscription |
| --- | --- |
| The `rfq:{rfq_id}` channel, the canonical one for `position:*` events | Yes. Subscribe to this one. |
| The `mm:quotes:{mm_id}` channel of the maker whose exposure closed | Yes |
| Any connection authenticated as the seller | No, it arrives on identity alone |
| Any connection authenticated as that maker | No |

The last two arrive on identity alone. If you also hold the `rfq:{rfq_id}` subscription, you may
see the same event twice. Dedupe on `tx_signature`.

The `position.bought_back` webhook carries the same moment.

### An acquiring bid notifies the buyer of nothing

<Warning>
One cashout outcome reaches the buyer on no channel: a bid that acquires a position instead of
closing it.

When the winning bid comes from a market maker that was not the position's counterparty, the
position transfers to them and stays open. There is no WebSocket event and no buyer-side webhook for
that. `position.transferred` goes to the seller.

If you bid on auctions for positions you do not already back, reconcile acquisitions from
[`GET /v1/rfqs`](/api-reference/parlays/list), where an acquired position appears on your account,
rather than waiting for a push.
</Warning>

Polling the RFQ status does not help either. A transfer leaves it `executed`, so polling for
`bought_back` never fires.

## Delivery guarantees

| Channel | Guarantee | On reconnect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SSE | Live only, for the life of the request | Resume with `last_event_id` |
| WebSocket | Live only, no redelivery | Reconcile against [read endpoints](/guides/reading-data) |
| Webhooks | At-least-once, signed, retried | [Replay](/guides/webhooks#replaying-deliveries) by delivery id |
| REST | Current truth, on demand | n/a |
