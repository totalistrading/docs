These pages are for platforms that embed Totalis trading in their own product: a terminal, a
wallet, a sports or prediction market app. They are not in the public navigation. Bookmark them and
share the links with your team.

## At a glance

- Same API, two integration models. The difference is who holds the Solana signing key.
- Funds sit in on-chain vaults in both models.
- Both models quote and settle the same way and use the same API key header. Headless adds a build, sign and submit step after commit.

## Choosing a model

In headless, one **principal** (your platform's Totalis account) registers many **sub-accounts**.
A sub-account is a Solana public key you prove you control, with its own vault.

| | Managed | Headless |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Status | Live | Preview, in active development |
| Accounts | One Totalis account per end user | One principal, many sub-accounts |
| Signing key | An embedded wallet whose key lives in a secure enclave | Your own key, or your end users' keys |
| Signing | The account delegates signing to Totalis once. Totalis signs inside the secure enclave | You sign each transaction Totalis builds |
| Onboarding | One consent in the Totalis app per account | A one time setup with the team for your principal and console access, then fully programmatic with proof of key control |
| Best for | A desk, a bot, or a small number of users | A platform that already holds keys for many users |

Pick managed, the fastest to ship, if every end user can hold a Totalis account and log in once.
Pick headless if your users never touch Totalis directly and you already hold their keys through
Privy, Turnkey, Fireblocks or your own signer.

## Shared basics

- Base URL: `https://api.totalis.trade`
- Authentication: an API key in the `X-API-Key` header on every request. Keys are scoped. See [Authentication](/guides/authentication).
- Markets, quoting, fees and settlement behave exactly as the public docs describe. Start with [How parlays work](/guides/lifecycle) and [Limits and fees](/guides/limits-and-fees).
- The public pages on [funding](/guides/funding), enabling trading and [delegated signing](/guides/vault-architecture#delegated-signing) describe the managed wallet. They do not apply to headless sub-accounts.
- Questions and onboarding: [founders@totalis.trade](mailto:founders@totalis.trade).
