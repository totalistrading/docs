Refer other traders and earn a cut of the fees they generate. Anyone who signs up through your
referral link is tied to your account, and you earn 20% of the trading fees on their trades.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Your share | 20% of the trading fee on each referred trade |
| Paid in | USDC, to the wallet linked to your account |
| Claim minimum | 25 USDC |
| Tiers | None. You earn only from people you refer directly. |

## How to refer someone

Open Settings and go to the Referrals tab. Copy your referral link and share it. When someone signs
up through that link they appear in your referred users list, and start earning you rewards the
moment they trade.

## What you earn

You earn 20% of the trading fee on every trade your referred users make. That is the fee charged
when a trade fills, so your reward is a straight cut of fees that were actually collected.

It builds up as a claimable balance in your account.

- Rewards accrue on both settled parlays and early cashouts, since both charge a trading fee.
- Your rate is fixed per reward at the time it accrues. A later rate change never touches what you
  have already earned.
- There are no extra tiers. You earn only from the people you refer directly, not from the people
  they refer.

## Claiming your rewards

The Referrals tab shows three numbers: what you have earned in total, what is available to claim
right now, and what you have already been paid.

Once your claimable balance reaches 25 USDC the Claim button turns on. Press it and the USDC is sent
to your wallet. The payout usually confirms within a few seconds, and the tab updates once it lands.

If your balance is under 25 USDC nothing is lost. It stays in your account and keeps growing as your
referred users trade. You can claim as soon as it clears the minimum.

## Before you claim

- Payouts go to the wallet linked to your account. Make sure one is set up.
- You can have one claim in flight at a time. If a payout is still processing, let it finish before
  starting another.
- A claim that sits as pending for a little while is safe. Your balance is never lost, and the
  payout completes once it settles.
