A withdrawal sends USDC from your Totalis balance to any address on HyperEVM, in one call to
[Create withdrawal](/hyperliquid/api-reference/withdrawals/create-withdrawal). Totalis relays it and pays
the gas.

<CodeGroup>
```ts SDK
const done = await totalis.withdraw({
  destination: "0x2222222222222222222222222222222222222222",
  amount: 15_000_000n, // 15 USDC
  onStarted: (withdrawalId) => save(withdrawalId), // persist it to follow after a restart
});
console.log(done.status, done.payout_transaction_hash); // PAYOUT_COMMITTED
```

```bash curl
curl https://hip4-api.totalis.trade/v1/withdrawals \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $TOTALIS_API_KEY" \
  -H "Idempotency-Key: 01998683-79f0-7c21-8d3e-5a6b7c8d9e0f" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "destination": "0x2222222222222222222222222222222222222222",
    "amount": "15000000",
    "vault_withdrawal": { "amount": "10000000", "expiry": "1790434800", "salt": "4919131752989213764", "signature": "0xefef...efef" },
    "payout_authorization": { "valid_after": "1790433540", "valid_before": "1790435100", "authorization_signature": "0x9e9e...9e1b" }
  }'
```
</CodeGroup>

The `totalis` client is created as on [Open a position](/hyperliquid/open-a-position#the-flow). The key
needs `funding:withdraw`, `sponsorship:use`, `account:read` and `account:stream`.

## What you sign

Your Totalis balance is USDC in your wallet plus your free vault balance. The payout always leaves from
your wallet, so a withdrawal has up to two signed parts, both in the one request:

| Part | When | You sign |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `vault_withdrawal` | Only when your wallet holds less than `amount`: moves exactly the shortfall from the vault to your wallet | EIP-712 `Exit` |
| `payout_authorization` | Always: pays exactly `amount` from your wallet to `destination` | USDC `TransferWithAuthorization` |

`withdraw` reads your balance and signs both parts for you. Without the SDK:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Read your balance">
    [Get balances](/hyperliquid/api-reference/account/get-balances). The shortfall is `amount` minus your
    spendable wallet USDC (`WALLET_USDC` minus `pending.wallet_reserved`, never below zero).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign the parts">
    With a shortfall, sign an `Exit` for exactly that amount with `exitTypedData`, with your wallet as
    both `account` and `to`. Generate the request's `Idempotency-Key`, derive the payout nonce with
    `payoutNonce(deployment, wallet, idempotencyKey)`, and sign the payout with
    `transferWithAuthorizationTypedData`. See [Signing](/hyperliquid/signing). The time windows are on
    [Limits](/hyperliquid/limits).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send it">
    Send `destination`, `amount`, `payout_authorization` and, with a shortfall, `vault_withdrawal`. The
    venue fills in your wallet. The `202` response is the withdrawal: admitted, not yet paid.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow it">
    Apply `WITHDRAWAL_UPDATED` events for your `withdrawal_id` on your
    [account subscription](/hyperliquid/account-stream). Each carries the whole withdrawal. Stop at
    `PAYOUT_COMMITTED` or `FAILED`. After a reconnect, read
    [Get withdrawal](/hyperliquid/api-reference/withdrawals/get-withdrawal) once.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The payout's USDC nonce derives from the request's `Idempotency-Key`, so the signature is bound to that
one request and cannot start a second withdrawal. A resend with the same key and body returns the same
withdrawal.

## Statuses

| `status` | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `VAULT_WITHDRAWAL_QUEUED` | The shortfall is moving from the vault to your wallet. The payout waits for it. |
| `PAYOUT_SUBMITTED` | The payout is queued or relayed. |
| `PAYOUT_COMMITTED` | Done. `destination` received `amount`, and `payout_transaction_hash` is set. |
| `FAILED` | Nothing left your Totalis balance. If the vault part committed, that USDC stays in your wallet. Read your balance and start a new withdrawal. |

## Survive a restart

`onStarted` receives the `withdrawal_id` once the venue admits the withdrawal. After a restart, follow
that withdrawal instead of starting a second one:

```ts
const done = await totalis.waitForWithdrawal("01998683-7a31-7e5a-a3c1-6f2b8d4e9a17");
```

To store the signed request before it leaves your process, split the call. `signWithdrawal` returns
`{ idempotencyKey, account, body }`, and `sendWithdrawal` sends it and follows it like `withdraw`.
Sending the same signed request again returns the same withdrawal.

```ts
const signed = await totalis.signWithdrawal({
  destination: "0x2222222222222222222222222222222222222222",
  amount: 15_000_000n,
});
await store.save(signed); // your storage: the key, your wallet and the exact body
const done = await totalis.sendWithdrawal(signed, { onStarted: (withdrawalId) => store.saveId(withdrawalId) });
```

## Errors to handle

| Code | Cause | Do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `STATE_CONFLICT` | Your wallet balance changed, so the signed shortfall no longer matches. | Read your balance and sign again under a new key. |
| `IDEMPOTENCY_KEY_REUSED` | The key was used with a different body. | Never reuse a key for a new withdrawal. |
| `FORBIDDEN` | The key lacks `funding:withdraw` or `sponsorship:use`. | Add the scope. |
| `FINANCIAL_ACTIONS_DISABLED` | Withdrawals are paused. | Retry after `Retry-After`. |

Every code is on [Errors](/hyperliquid/errors).
