Totalis lets you trade Hyperliquid's HIP-4 outcome markets as singles or combos. A **single** is one
outcome and trades on the HyperCore order book. A **combo** is several outcomes in one position that
pays in full only if every leg wins. Fractional results can pay part of that amount. Combos are request-for-quote:
market makers compete to quote yours. You sign the quote you choose and any required funding authorizations.
The maker confirms, then Totalis submits the trade to its vault on HyperEVM.

You can also put Totalis in your own frontend, where your users trade with their own wallets:
[Build on Totalis](/hyperliquid/build/overview).

| Position | Execution | Funds and API |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Native single | A YES or NO token on HyperCore's order book. | HyperCore spot cash and Hyperliquid's [exchange API](https://hyperliquid.gitbook.io/hyperliquid-docs/for-developers/api/exchange-endpoint). |
| One-leg RFQ position | A maker quote accepted through Totalis. | Totalis vault position, signatures and fees, using the RFQ API below. |
| Combo | One Totalis RFQ position with several legs. | The same RFQ API and vault flow, with a product of the legs' payout factors. |

A one-leg RFQ is not a native order-book order. The Totalis RFQ API does not place or cancel HyperCore
orders. [Balances and funding](/hyperliquid/guides/funding) explains how to move cash between the two.

## Try it without an account

Market data is public. This call needs no key and no signup:

```bash
curl "https://hip4-api.totalis.trade/v1/markets?limit=3"
```

Market reads, RFQ reads and [Get deployment](/hyperliquid/api-reference/deployment/get-deployment) are public. Every
other account, trading and maker route needs an API key or a [wallet session](/hyperliquid/build/partner-frontend#sessions). Start with the [Quickstart](/hyperliquid/quickstart).

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Ask for quotes">
    [Create an RFQ](/hyperliquid/api-reference/rfqs/create-rfq) with your legs and a stake. Every
    maker sees it at once.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Makers compete">
    Each maker answers with a `payout`: what the position pays if every leg wins. Quotes reach you
    live on the [WebSocket](/hyperliquid/guides/realtime).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Accept and fill">
    Sign the quote you want and [accept it](/hyperliquid/api-reference/quotes/accept-quote). The maker
    can confirm or decline within the [confirm window](/hyperliquid/limits-and-fees). An `ACCEPTED`
    response queues the trade. Its operation reaching `COMMITTED` establishes the on-chain fill.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Settle, or cash out first">
    Totalis submits eligible settlements and claims automatically. Returns first credit your vault balance.
    [Settlement and returns](/hyperliquid/guides/settlement) explains fractional results, delayed claims and
    exceptional outcomes. To leave early, request a [cash-out](/hyperliquid/guides/trading#cash-out).
    It needs an executable bid and is not guaranteed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Both sides of a trade

| Step | Taker | Maker |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | [Create RFQ](/hyperliquid/api-reference/rfqs/create-rfq): legs and a stake. | Receives `RFQ_CREATED`. |
| 2 | Receives `QUOTE_CREATED`. | [Create quote](/hyperliquid/api-reference/quotes/create-quote): the payout. |
| 3 | [Accept quote](/hyperliquid/api-reference/quotes/accept-quote) with a signature. | Receives `QUOTE_ACCEPTED`. |
| 4 | The accept response carries the maker's answer. | [Confirm quote](/hyperliquid/api-reference/quotes/confirm-quote) with a signature, or decline. |
| 5 | Holds the position once the operation is `COMMITTED`. | Backs it from the maker vault. |

Your USDC sits in your Totalis wallet and the vault contract. The vault moves funds only with a
signature from the funds' owner. You do not need to deposit before you trade: an accept with signed `funding` pulls any shortfall from your
wallet, and the SDK adds it for you. [Funding](/hyperliquid/guides/funding) shows where cash sits before and
after trading. [Contract interfaces](/hyperliquid/guides/contracts) describes the vault's controls and direct calls.

## The essentials

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Base URL | `https://hip4-api.totalis.trade`, on HyperEVM (chain ID `999`) |
| Auth | `Authorization: Bearer <key>` ([Authentication](/hyperliquid/authentication)). Market reads and the deployment need no key. |
| Staging | `https://hip4-api-staging.totalis.trade`, on HyperEVM testnet (chain ID `998`): [Staging](/hyperliquid/build/staging) |
| Amounts | Trading amounts are micro-USDC strings: `"25000000"` is 25 USDC. Activity effects use their asset's own decimals ([conventions](/hyperliquid/api-reference/introduction#requests)). |
| Gas | Totalis pays it for takers. Makers pay their own, in HYPE. |
| Separate from Solana | The [Solana API](/solana/quickstart) has its own keys and endpoints |

## What you can build

| Build | Start with | Constraint |
| --- | --- | --- |
| An own-wallet combo frontend | [Partner wallets](/hyperliquid/build/partner-frontend) and [Frontend lifecycle](/hyperliquid/build/frontend-lifecycle) | Partner access, registered origins and one wallet identity per account. |
| An algorithmic taker | [API trading](/hyperliquid/guides/api-trading) | Quote acceptance also needs the account wallet's signature. |
| A market maker | [Market making](/hyperliquid/guides/market-making) | Maker collateral and your own HYPE for chain transactions. |
| A combo feature in a Hyperliquid terminal | [Funding](/hyperliquid/guides/funding) | Native orders and Totalis positions have separate execution and cash locations. |
| A portfolio or capital dashboard | [Account reads](/hyperliquid/api-reference/account/get-balances) and [Maker collateral](/hyperliquid/guides/collateral) | Respect freshness, unknown amounts and asset units. |
| A partner distribution channel | [Builder codes](/hyperliquid/build/builder-codes) | Attribution follows admission and signed-origin rules. |

Market reference prices are not executable combo prices. A maker prices the combined exposure, including
correlation. Multiplying displayed chances does not establish an executable payout or a guaranteed hedge.
