Needs `funding:withdraw` and `sponsorship:use`.

```bash
curl https://hip4-api.totalis.trade/v1/withdrawals \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $TOTALIS_API_KEY" \
  -H "Idempotency-Key: 01998683-79f0-7c21-8d3e-5a6b7c8d9e0f" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "destination": "0x2222222222222222222222222222222222222222",
    "amount": "15000000",
    "vault_withdrawal": { "amount": "10000000", "expiry": "1790434800", "salt": "4919131752989213764", "signature": "0xefef...efef" },
    "payout_authorization": { "valid_after": "1790433540", "valid_before": "1790435100", "authorization_signature": "0x9e9e...9e1b" }
  }'
```

## What you sign

The payout always leaves from your wallet.

| Part | When | Message |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `vault_withdrawal` | Your wallet holds less than `amount`. Moves exactly the shortfall from the vault to your wallet. | `Exit`, with your wallet as `account` and `to` |
| `payout_authorization` | Always. Pays `amount` from your wallet to `destination`. | USDC `TransferWithAuthorization` |

The shortfall is `amount` minus `WALLET_USDC` minus `pending.wallet_reserved` in
[Get balances](/hyperliquid/api-reference/account/get-balances), never below zero. The payout nonce
derives from the `Idempotency-Key` ([USDC nonces](/hyperliquid/authentication#usdc-nonces)), so choose the
key before you sign.

## Statuses

`202` means admitted, not paid. Follow `WITHDRAWAL_UPDATED` on the
[account channel](/hyperliquid/websocket/account).

| `status` | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `VAULT_WITHDRAWAL_QUEUED` | The shortfall is moving to your wallet. |
| `PAYOUT_SUBMITTED` | The payout is queued or relayed. |
| `PAYOUT_COMMITTED` | Done. `payout_transaction_hash` is set. |
| `FAILED` | Nothing left your Totalis balance. USDC already moved from the vault stays in your wallet. |

A resend with the same key and body returns the same withdrawal. A `409 STATE_CONFLICT` means your
balance changed: re-read it and sign again under a new key.
