**You do nothing.** When a position's legs resolve, Totalis settles it on-chain and claims any winnings
into the owner's vault balance. There is no route to call, no scope to hold and nothing to sign. Both
contract calls are permissionless and always pay the owner, so Totalis runs them for every position. How
often it runs them is on [Limits](/hyperliquid/limits).

## What you see

Your [account subscription](/hyperliquid/account-stream) reports each step. Read the position again on
each event to see its new status and amounts.

| Event | Meaning | Position `status` after |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `POSITION_SETTLED` | The position was graded. Any amount up to your net stake is credited to your vault balance now. | `SETTLED_UNCLAIMED` when there is profit to claim, otherwise `CLOSED` |
| `POSITION_CLOSED` | The profit was claimed into your vault balance (`close_reason: CLAIM`), or the position was sold back (`close_reason: SELL_BACK`). | `CLOSED` |
| `VAULT_BALANCE_CHANGED` | Your vault balance changed. | |

<CodeGroup>
```ts SDK
totalis.stream.on("event", async (event) => {
  if (event.event_type !== "POSITION_SETTLED" && event.event_type !== "POSITION_CLOSED") return;
  const { position } = unwrap(await totalis.client.GET("/v1/me/positions/{position_id}", {
    params: { path: { position_id: event.payload.position_id } },
  }));
  console.log(position.status, position.settlement_kind, position.settlement_payout);
});
```

```bash curl
curl https://hip4-api.totalis.trade/v1/me/positions/$POSITION_ID \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $TOTALIS_API_KEY"
```
</CodeGroup>

## Position statuses

| `status` | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `OPEN` | At least one leg is unresolved. You can [cash out](/hyperliquid/cash-out). |
| `AWAITING_SETTLEMENT` | Every leg resolved, or a leg lost, and settlement has not landed yet. |
| `SETTLED_UNCLAIMED` | Settled as a `WIN` with profit Totalis has not claimed yet. |
| `CLOSED` | Claimed (`close_reason: CLAIM`), sold back (`close_reason: SELL_BACK`), or settled with no profit to claim (`close_reason: null`). |

A position that was transferred to another maker in a cash-out leaves your position list: its owner
changed.

## Settlement kinds

| `settlement_kind` | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `WIN` | Every leg resolved and the result pays at least your net stake. The net stake is credited at settlement and any profit is claimed after. |
| `PARTIAL` | Every leg resolved and the result pays something, but less than your net stake. It is credited at settlement, with nothing to claim. |
| `LOSS` | Every leg resolved and the result pays nothing. |
| `EARLY_LOST` | A leg lost while others were unresolved, so the position settled early. It pays nothing. |

Each leg carries `result_numerator` over `result_denominator`: the full denominator is a win, `0` a
loss, and anything between is a fractional result. The position's result is its `payout` times the
product of its legs' results, so `WIN` does not mean every leg paid in full.

## Amounts

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `net_stake` | The stake after the entry fee. |
| `payout` | What the position pays if every leg wins, before the profit fee. |
| `settlement_payout` | What settlement awarded, before the profit fee. |
| `unclaimed_profit` | Profit (settlement payout minus net stake) still to be claimed. |
| `fee_bps` | The profit fee, charged on that profit at claim. |

Amounts are atomic USDC strings, or `null` when they cannot be established. Each read carries one
`context` with `as_of` and a `status` of `CURRENT`, `STALE` or `UNAVAILABLE`. Current fee values are in
[Get deployment](/hyperliquid/api-reference/deployment/get-deployment).

Claimed winnings land in your vault balance. They fund your next position, or leave with a
[withdrawal](/hyperliquid/withdraw).
