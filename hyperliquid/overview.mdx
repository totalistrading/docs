Totalis lets you trade Hyperliquid HIP-4 prediction markets, one outcome (a single) or several (a
parlay), at a price market makers quote. You accept a quote with a wallet signature, and the position
settles on-chain in the Totalis vault on HyperEVM. This API is separate from the
[Solana API](/guides/quickstart) and has its own keys.

## How an RFQ works

| Step | Taker | Maker |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | [Create RFQ](/hyperliquid/api-reference/rfqs/create-rfq): legs and a stake. | Receives `RFQ_CREATED`. |
| 2 | Receives `QUOTE_CREATED`. | [Create quote](/hyperliquid/api-reference/quotes/create-quote): the payout. |
| 3 | [Accept quote](/hyperliquid/api-reference/quotes/accept-quote) with a signature. | Receives `QUOTE_ACCEPTED`. |
| 4 | The accept response carries the maker's answer. | [Confirm quote](/hyperliquid/api-reference/quotes/confirm-quote) with a signature, or decline. |
| 5 | Holds the position. | Backs it from the maker vault. |

Totalis executes, settles and claims every position. There is no deposit step and no claim step.
Guides: [Trading](/hyperliquid/trading) and [Market making](/hyperliquid/market-making).

## Base URL

| Environment | URL | Chain |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Production | `https://hip4-api.totalis.trade` | HyperEVM, chain ID `999` |
| Staging | `https://hip4-api-staging.totalis.trade` | HyperEVM testnet, chain ID `998` |

Send `Authorization: Bearer <key>` ([Authentication](/hyperliquid/authentication)). Market reads need
no key. Amounts are strings in USDC atomic units: `"25000000"` is 25 USDC.

## Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Market | One HIP-4 outcome (`outcome_id`) with a `YES` and a `NO` side. |
| Leg | One outcome and one side: `{ "outcome_id": "1209", "side": "YES" }`. One leg is a single; two or more is a parlay. |
| Taker | The account that creates an RFQ and accepts a quote. For a cash-out, the position's owner. |
| Maker | A market maker (`maker_id`) that quotes, confirms and backs positions from its maker vault. |
| RFQ | Request for quote. `ENTRY` prices new legs for a stake; `CASHOUT` prices an existing position. |
| Quote | A maker's unsigned offer on one RFQ: `entry` terms (a `payout`), or `sell_back` or `transfer` terms (a `price`). |
| Acceptance | The record of one accept on one quote, named by the accept's `Idempotency-Key` (`acceptance_id`). A quote has at most one. |
| Confirm | The maker's answer to an acceptance before its `deadline`: `CONFIRM` with a signature, or `DECLINE`. |
| Position | A filled trade on-chain: legs, stake, payout, owner and backing maker. An entry position's `position_id` is its `quote_id`. |
| Stake, payout | What the taker pays, before the entry fee; what the position pays if every leg wins, before the profit fee. |
| Cash-out | Selling an open position: a `sell_back` to its maker, or a `transfer` to another maker. |
| Settlement, claim | Grading a resolved position, then paying its winnings into the owner's vault balance. Totalis runs both. |
| Operation | One on-chain action Totalis relays for you, with a `status` (`operation_id`, `OPERATION_UPDATED`). |
| Vault | The Totalis contract on HyperEVM that holds USDC and every position (`vault_address`). |
| Totalis balance | Your wallet's USDC on HyperEVM plus your free vault balance. |
| Wallet | Your Totalis embedded wallet. It approves your keys and signs your trades. |
| Deployment | The chain, vault, fees and USDC domain every signature binds to ([Get deployment](/hyperliquid/api-reference/deployment/get-deployment)). |
| Cursor | Your place in the [WebSocket](/hyperliquid/websocket/connection). Send it back to resume. |
