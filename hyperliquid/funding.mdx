Your Totalis balance on HyperEVM has two parts:

| Part | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Wallet** (`WALLET_USDC`) | USDC held by your Totalis wallet, the embedded wallet in `GET /v1/me`. |
| **Vault** (`VAULT_FREE`) | Your unreserved USDC inside the Totalis vault contract. Stakes are taken from it, and payouts and cashouts are paid into it. |

Totalis pays the gas for funding a bet and for withdrawals, so your wallet needs no HYPE. Every
movement of money also carries a signature from your wallet, which the SDK makes with the signer
you pass. The examples use the `totalis` client from [Trading](/hyperliquid/trading#1-create-a-client).

| To | SDK | Key scopes | You sign |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Fund a bet from your wallet | `placeBet` (by default) | `funding:deposit` and `sponsorship:use`, with the taker bot scopes | USDC `ReceiveWithAuthorization` for the vault shortfall |
| Send USDC to any address | `withdraw` | `funding:withdraw`, `sponsorship:use`, `account:read`, `account:stream` | Vault `Exit` for any wallet shortfall, and USDC `TransferWithAuthorization` for the payout |

## Read your balance

[Get balances](/hyperliquid/api-reference/account/get-balances) returns one balance, `TOTALIS`:

```ts
import { unwrap } from "@totalistrading/hip4-client";

const { balances: [totalisBalance] } = unwrap(await totalis.client.GET("/v1/me/balances"));
console.log(totalisBalance?.amount, totalisBalance?.components); // WALLET_USDC and VAULT_FREE
```

```json
{
  "balances": [
    {
      "product": "TOTALIS",
      "amount": "37500000",
      "unit": "USDC_BASE_UNITS",
      "state": "CURRENT",
      "components": [
        { "kind": "WALLET_USDC", "amount": "12500000", "state": "CURRENT" },
        { "kind": "VAULT_FREE", "amount": "30000000", "state": "CURRENT" }
      ],
      "pending": {
        "wallet_reserved": "5000000",
        "vault_deposits": "0",
        "vault_withdrawals": "0",
        "core_exits": "0",
        "has_unknown_operations": false
      }
    }
  ],
  "checkpoint": { "chain_id": "999", "block_number": "31552210", "projection_state": "CURRENT" }
}
```

`amount` is what you can spend: wallet USDC plus vault USDC, minus wallet USDC already reserved by
pending work, never below zero. It is `null` unless `state` is `CURRENT`. The response holds the
Totalis balance only. HyperCore balances are not part of it: read them from Hyperliquid.

## Get USDC into your wallet

Send native USDC on HyperEVM (chain ID `999`) to your wallet address. The token is
`0xb88339cb7199b77e23db6e890353e22632ba630f`, the `usdc.address` in
[Get vault release](/hyperliquid/api-reference/vault/get-vault-release). To fund from another chain, deposit in the
[Totalis app](https://totalis-hip4.vercel.app), which bridges it for you.

## Fund a bet

There is no separate deposit. When you accept a quote and your vault balance is short of the stake,
`placeBet` signs a USDC `ReceiveWithAuthorization` for exactly the shortfall and attaches it to the
accept as `funding`. The vault draws it from your wallet in the same trade.

| `funding` option | What `placeBet` does |
| --- | --- |
| `"wallet"` (default) | Signs an authorization for any vault shortfall. Nothing is signed when the vault covers the stake. |
| `"none"` | Never funds. The vault balance must cover the stake. |

The accept needs `funding:deposit` only when it carries a funding authorization. Leave the scope
off a key that should only spend what is already in the vault.

Without the SDK, build the authorization with `receiveWithAuthorizationTypedData(release, authorization)`
from `@totalistrading/hip4-client/typed-data`, sign it with your wallet, and send the authorization
fields plus `authorization_signature`, `quote_digest`, and `taker_signature` as `funding` in
[Accept quote](/hyperliquid/api-reference/rfqs-and-quotes/accept-quote). The builder derives the
USDC nonce from your `salt`, your address, and the vault, exactly as the vault checks it. The
authorization window rules are on [Limits and timing](/hyperliquid/conventions#limits-and-timing).

## Withdraw to any address

```ts
const done = await totalis.withdraw({
  destination: "0x2222222222222222222222222222222222222222",
  amount: 15_000_000n, // 15 USDC
  onStarted: (withdrawalId) => console.log("save", withdrawalId), // persist it to follow after a restart
});
console.log(done.state, done.payout_transaction_hash); // PAYOUT_COMMITTED
totalis.close();
```

A withdrawal pays out from your wallet, and it is one signed request. `withdraw` reads your balance
and signs both parts up front:

| Part | When | You sign |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `vault_withdrawal` | Only when the wallet cannot cover `amount`: moves exactly the shortfall from the vault to your own wallet | Vault `Exit` |
| `payout_authorization` | Always: pays exactly `amount` from your wallet to `destination` | USDC `TransferWithAuthorization` |

It sends both in one [Withdraw to an external address](/hyperliquid/api-reference/funds/withdraw-to-an-external-address)
request. Totalis relays the vault leg, relays the payout once the vault leg commits, and pays the
gas for both. `withdraw` resolves at `PAYOUT_COMMITTED` from `WITHDRAWAL_UPDATED` events on your
account stream, and throws at `FAILED`. It reads
[Get withdrawal](/hyperliquid/api-reference/recovery/get-withdrawal) only after a reconnect.

| `state` | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `VAULT_WITHDRAWAL_QUEUED` | The vault shortfall is moving to your wallet. The payout waits for it. |
| `PAYOUT_SUBMITTED` | The payout is queued or relayed. |
| `PAYOUT_COMMITTED` | Done. `destination` received `amount`, and `payout_transaction_hash` is set. |
| `FAILED` | The withdrawal stopped and nothing left your Totalis balance. A vault leg that committed stays in your wallet. Read your balance and start a new one. |

`onStarted` receives the `withdrawal_id` once the venue admits the withdrawal. If your process
restarts, follow the same withdrawal instead of starting a second one:

```ts
const done = await totalis.waitForWithdrawal("01998683-7a31-7e5a-a3c1-6f2b8d4e9a17");
```

### Keep a signed withdrawal across a restart

`withdraw` signs and sends in one call. To store the signed request before it leaves your process,
split it: `signWithdrawal` returns `{ idempotencyKey, body }`, and `sendWithdrawal` sends it and
follows it like `withdraw`. Sending the same `{ idempotencyKey, body }` again, after a lost response
or a restart, returns the same withdrawal, never a second one.

```ts
const signed = await totalis.signWithdrawal({
  destination: "0x2222222222222222222222222222222222222222",
  amount: 15_000_000n,
});
await store.save(signed); // your storage: the key and the exact body
const done = await totalis.sendWithdrawal(signed, { onStarted: (withdrawalId) => store.saveId(withdrawalId) });
```

The payout's USDC nonce derives from the request's `Idempotency-Key`, so the signature is bound to
that one request and cannot start a second withdrawal. The SDK signs a payout only for the
destination and amount you asked for.

### Without the SDK

<Steps>
  <Step title="Read your balance">
    [Get balances](/hyperliquid/api-reference/account/get-balances). The shortfall is `amount` minus
    your spendable wallet USDC (`WALLET_USDC` minus `pending.wallet_reserved`, never below zero).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign the parts">
    If there is a shortfall, sign an `Exit` from the vault to your own wallet for exactly that
    amount with `exitTypedData`. Then generate the request's `Idempotency-Key`, compute the payout
    nonce with `payoutNonce(release, account, idempotencyKey)`, and sign the payout with
    `transferWithAuthorizationTypedData(release, { from, to, value, valid_after, valid_before, nonce })`.
    The window rules are on [Limits and timing](/hyperliquid/conventions#limits-and-timing).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send it">
    [Withdraw to an external address](/hyperliquid/api-reference/funds/withdraw-to-an-external-address)
    with `account`, `destination`, `amount`, `vault_withdrawal` (only with a shortfall), and
    `payout_authorization` (`valid_after`, `valid_before`, `authorization_signature`). The `202`
    response is the withdrawal, admitted but not paid. A shortfall that no longer matches returns
    `STATE_CONFLICT`: re-read your balance and sign again under a new key.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow it">
    Apply `WITHDRAWAL_UPDATED` events for your `withdrawal_id`. Each carries the whole withdrawal.
    Stop at `PAYOUT_COMMITTED` or `FAILED`. After a reconnect, read
    [Get withdrawal](/hyperliquid/api-reference/recovery/get-withdrawal) once.
  </Step>
</Steps>
