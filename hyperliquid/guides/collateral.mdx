Every position you confirm is backed by your maker vault. When you fill, the vault locks that
position's worst case: what you pay if every leg wins. Positions that cannot all win at once still
lock in full until you run a **collateral reduction**. A reduction lowers your requirement to what
your whole book can lose together.

<img className="hidden dark:block rounded-2xl" src="/images/pictures/hl-collateral-dark.png" alt="Two positions on the same tournament, only one of which can win: backed one by one they lock 13,000, reduced they lock 8,000" />

In the picture you back two positions on one tournament winner market. Only one team can win. Backed
one by one they lock 13,000 USDC. After a reduction they lock 8,000, the larger of the two.

## Your capital

[Get maker capital](/hyperliquid/api-reference/makers/get-maker-capital) reads the maker vault
exactly as the contract sees it. All amounts are atomic USDC.

```bash
curl https://hip4-api.totalis.trade/v1/makers/$MAKER_ID/capital \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $TOTALIS_MAKER_KEY"
```

```json
{
  "context": { "as_of": "2026-09-26T12:00:02Z", "status": "CURRENT" },
  "owner": "0xaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaa",
  "signer": "0xaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaa",
  "vault_gross": "5250000000",
  "stake_escrow": "150000000",
  "payable_owed": "40000000",
  "gross_open": "300000000",
  "discount": "0",
  "requirement": "300000000",
  "free_collateral": "4760000000",
  "capital_net_of_senior_liabilities": "5060000000",
  "floor_bps": 0,
  "pending_commitments": "25000000",
  "free_after_pending": "4735000000",
  "deployment_digest": "0x40452ed111ecbd2a2c5bc4200002747a19eb0f8d62e602c38e34204d3f609910",
  "actions": [
    { "action": "MAKER_WITHDRAW", "route": "MAKER_VAULT", "available": "4735000000", "blockers": [] },
    { "action": "MAKER_QUOTE", "route": "MAKER_VAULT", "available": null, "blockers": ["PRIVATE_QUOTE_CAPACITY_UNAVAILABLE"] }
  ],
  "reservations": [
    { "reservation_id": "entry:0xb2f6...f013", "operation_ids": ["0x1ba1...9c8c"], "value": "25000000", "status": "ACTIVE" }
  ]
}
```

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `vault_gross` | All USDC in your maker vault, including takers' stakes held in it. |
| `stake_escrow` | Takers' net stakes on your open positions. You keep them if the positions lose. |
| `payable_owed` | Winnings you owe on settled positions that are not yet claimed. |
| `gross_open` | The sum of `payout - net stake` over your open positions: your worst case, position by position. |
| `discount` | What your last collateral reductions took off `gross_open`. |
| `requirement` | What must stay locked for your open positions: `max(gross_open - min(discount, gross_open), ceil(gross_open * floor_bps / 10000))`. With a `floor_bps` of 0 that is `gross_open - discount`. `null` when `floor_bps` is `null`. |
| `floor_bps` | The share of `gross_open` that stays locked whatever the discount. `null` when it cannot be read. |
| `free_collateral` | `vault_gross - stake_escrow - payable_owed - requirement`, never below zero. What can back new fills, or be withdrawn. |
| `capital_net_of_senior_liabilities` | `vault_gross - stake_escrow - payable_owed`, signed. It does not value your open positions. |
| `pending_commitments` | Fills you confirmed that are not yet on-chain: the sum of `reservations`. |
| `free_after_pending` | `free_collateral - pending_commitments`, never below zero. Size new quotes from this. |
| `reservations` | One row per confirmed fill not yet on-chain. `status` `UNKNOWN` means its outcome is not known yet; it still counts. |
| `actions` | `MAKER_WITHDRAW`: the most a new [withdrawal request](#move-maker-funds) may ask for, or `null` with a blocker such as `MAKER_WITHDRAW_PENDING`. `MAKER_QUOTE` is always `null`: only your bot knows its other live quotes. |
| `deployment_digest` | The hash of the deployment this read is bound to. |
| `signer` | Your maker's quote signer. It equals `owner`: a maker is one wallet. The zero address means the maker cannot quote. |
| `context.status` | `CURRENT`, or `STALE` or `UNAVAILABLE` when a source lags. A stale read never authorizes an action. |

Every amount is `null` when it cannot be read, never `0` in its place.

Re-read capital on every `MAKER_CAPITAL_UPDATED` on the [maker channel](/hyperliquid/api-reference/websocket/maker).

## How each step moves it

| When | What changes |
| --- | --- |
| You confirm a fill | The taker's net stake joins `vault_gross` and `stake_escrow`. `gross_open` rises by the position's `payout - net stake`. `discount` does not change, so the new position locks in full. |
| A position loses | You keep the stake: it leaves `stake_escrow` and stays in `vault_gross`. `gross_open` falls by the position's worst case. |
| A position wins | The taker's stake goes back to them. You owe the profit, held in `payable_owed` until Totalis claims it for the taker. `gross_open` falls. |
| A position pays part (`PARTIAL`) | What it pays leaves `vault_gross` for the owner, and you keep the rest of the stake. It leaves `stake_escrow`. `gross_open` falls. |
| A position closes either way | `discount` falls by the part of the closed position's worst case you did not pay, so the credit it earned leaves with it. |
| You buy a position back (`SELL_BACK`) | The price comes out of `vault_gross`. The net stake leaves `stake_escrow`, so it is yours again. The position closes as above. |
| Another maker buys it (`TRANSFER`) | Nothing changes for you. You still back it, for its new owner. |
| You deposit or withdraw | `vault_gross` moves. A withdrawal must leave `free_collateral` covered. |

The profit fee comes out of the taker's winnings, not your vault. The entry fee comes out of the
taker's stake before it reaches you. Rates are on [Limits](/hyperliquid/limits-and-fees).

## When collateral is checked

A quote reserves nothing until you confirm it. From your confirm until the fill lands on-chain or its
`expiry` passes, the fill holds what it can cost you against your capacity.

| Confirm | Holds |
| --- | --- |
| Entry | Its worst case, `payout - net stake`. |
| `SELL_BACK` | Its `price`, less the position's net stake and the worst case it releases. Never below zero: a buyback that frees capital holds nothing. |
| `TRANSFER` | Its `price`, against your wallet's own vault balance, not the maker vault. |

Each entry and `SELL_BACK` confirm is checked against `free_collateral` less the fills you confirmed that are not yet on-chain. A `TRANSFER` confirm is checked against your wallet's own vault balance instead. If
several of your quotes are accepted at once, a later confirm can fail with `QUOTE_CAPACITY_EXCEEDED`, and
nothing fills. Quote only what `free_after_pending` covers. A maker whose free capital is short of its
requirement, but whose vault still covers stakes and owed winnings, can still buy back a position whose close
restores its coverage.

The contract checks again when the trade lands, and a fill it refuses ends `FAILED`. That can still happen
after `ACCEPTED`: on an RFQ without `intent_id`, the confirm check is skipped while Totalis's view of the chain
lags, and the vault decides.

A pending maker withdrawal reserves nothing. Fills during its delay can use that USDC, and executing the
withdrawal then reverts. While one is pending, subtract its `pending.amount`
([Get maker withdrawal](/hyperliquid/api-reference/makers/get-maker-withdrawal)) from what you quote with.

## Reduce collateral

A reduction looks at every open position you back, finds the most your book can lose under any way
the markets can resolve, and certifies the difference as your `discount`. You submit that
certificate on-chain from a wallet that pays the gas. Totalis does not run reductions for you, and
it does not pay their gas. It runs headless: the API calls use a maker key with the
[maker collateral scopes](/hyperliquid/authentication#scopes) (`COLLATERAL_SCOPES` in the SDK), and a wallet that
holds HYPE signs the certificate transaction. Your maker wallet works.

```ts
import { createMaker } from "@totalistrading/hip4-client/maker";
import { createWalletClient, defineChain, http } from "viem";
import { privateKeyToAccount } from "viem/accounts";

const wallet = privateKeyToAccount(process.env.TOTALIS_WALLET_KEY as `0x${string}`);
const hyperEvm = defineChain({
  id: 999, name: "HyperEVM", nativeCurrency: { name: "HYPE", symbol: "HYPE", decimals: 18 },
  rpcUrls: { default: { http: ["https://rpc.hyperliquid.xyz/evm"] } },
});
const walletClient = createWalletClient({ account: wallet, chain: hyperEvm, transport: http() });
const maker = createMaker({
  environment: "production", apiKey: process.env.TOTALIS_COLLATERAL_KEY!, makerId: process.env.TOTALIS_MAKER_ID!, signer: wallet,
});
const { job, submitted } = await maker.reduceCollateral({
  signTransaction: async (call) =>
    walletClient.signTransaction(await walletClient.prepareTransactionRequest({ to: call.to, data: call.data, value: 0n })),
});
```

`id` is `chain_id` in [Get deployment](/hyperliquid/api-reference/deployment/get-deployment). The steps below are the
same flow over the API.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a job">
    [Create collateral reduction](/hyperliquid/api-reference/makers/create-collateral-reduction).
    It takes no body. You get a `job_id` and `next_eligible_at`, the earliest time a new discount
    can land.

    ```bash
    curl -X POST https://hip4-api.totalis.trade/v1/makers/$MAKER_ID/collateral-reductions \
      -H "Authorization: Bearer $TOTALIS_MAKER_KEY" \
      -H "Idempotency-Key: 01998713-2f4a-7c3e-9b1d-6a0e4f5c8d20"
    ```

    ```json
    {
      "job_id": "01998713-2f4a-7c3e-9b1d-6a0e4f5c8d21",
      "status": "QUEUED",
      "next_eligible_at": "2026-09-26T14:07:00Z",
      "status_url": "/v1/makers/7/collateral-reductions/01998713-2f4a-7c3e-9b1d-6a0e4f5c8d21"
    }
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for the certificate">
    Follow `COLLATERAL_REDUCTION_UPDATED` on the maker channel, or poll
    [Get collateral reduction](/hyperliquid/api-reference/makers/get-collateral-reduction), until
    `status` is `DURABLE`. The job then holds a `transaction`: `to`, `data`, `value` and `chain_id`
    for one call to the vault.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign and submit it">
    Sign that call as an EIP-1559 (type 2) transaction to the vault on this chain, with zero value and
    exactly the job's calldata. Any wallet that holds HYPE for gas can send it; your maker wallet works. Send it to
    [Submit self-funded transaction](/hyperliquid/api-reference/makers/submit-self-funded-transaction)
    with the job's `operation_id` and the same `Idempotency-Key` that created the job, before the
    job's `expiry`. Totalis checks the transaction matches the job and broadcasts it. It never holds
    that wallet's key or picks its nonce.

    ```bash
    curl https://hip4-api.totalis.trade/v1/makers/$MAKER_ID/operations/$OPERATION_ID/self-funded-transaction \
      -H "Authorization: Bearer $TOTALIS_MAKER_KEY" \
      -H "Idempotency-Key: 01998713-2f4a-7c3e-9b1d-6a0e4f5c8d20" \
      -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
      -d '{ "signed_transaction": "0x02f8d4..." }'
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Collect the headroom">
    When the transaction lands, `MAKER_CAPITAL_UPDATED` follows. `discount` is higher,
    `requirement` lower, and `free_collateral` higher by the same amount. No funds move: the freed
    USDC stays in your maker vault, ready to back fills or be withdrawn.
  </Step>
</Steps>

| Job `status` | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `QUEUED` | Waiting for `next_eligible_at`. |
| `SOLVING` | Computing your book's worst case. |
| `DURABLE` | The certificate is signed and `transaction` is ready. Your requirement drops only once it lands. |
| `NO_IMPROVEMENT` | Your book has nothing to net. Nothing to submit. |
| `FAILED` | No certificate. `failure_code` says why. Start a new job. |

- One job runs per maker at a time. Creating another while one is open returns the open one.
- A new discount only ever raises the old one. It never undoes a larger discount still in force.
- New fills do not use the discount, and closing positions use it up. Run a reduction again after
  your book grows. [The TypeScript SDK](/hyperliquid/sdk#make-markets)'s `reduceCollateral` runs all
  four steps.
- `COLLATERAL_REDUCTION_UNAVAILABLE` means reductions are switched off for this vault right now.
  Retry later.

### What nets

The reduction treats these positions as linked, and every other pair as independent:

| Linked | Why |
| --- | --- |
| The legs of one position | It pays only if every leg wins. |
| `YES` and `NO` of the same outcome | Only one side can win. |
| Outcomes of one tournament winner question | Only one named outcome can win. |

Treating two markets as independent can only overstate your worst case, never understate it. The
certificate never lowers your requirement below what your book can actually lose.

### Undo a discount

Your wallet can call `resetDiscount` on the vault at any time. It sets `discount` to zero, so every
open position locks in full again. It can only raise your requirement, never lower it.

## Move maker funds

Your wallet calls the vault directly and pays the gas in HYPE. Totalis does not relay these, and /mm
only shows a pending withdrawal: [Fund and withdraw](/hyperliquid/guides/market-making#fund-and-withdraw)
runs each call headless with the SDK. The
vault is `vault_address` and USDC is `usdc.address` in
[Get deployment](/hyperliquid/api-reference/deployment/get-deployment). Every `amount` is atomic USDC
(6 decimals).

```solidity
interface ITotalisMakerVault {
    function depositMaker(uint64 makerId, address expectedController, uint128 amount) external;
    function requestMakerWithdraw(uint64 makerId, uint128 amount, address to) external;
    function executeMakerWithdraw(uint64 makerId) external;
    function cancelMakerWithdraw(uint64 makerId) external;
    function resetDiscount(uint64 makerId) external;

    function makerOf(address controller) external view returns (uint64 makerId);
    function makerWithdrawals(uint64 makerId) external view returns (uint128 amount, address to, uint64 readyAt);

    event MakerDeposited(uint64 indexed makerId, address payer, uint128 amount);
    event MakerWithdrawRequested(uint64 indexed makerId, uint128 amount, address to, uint64 readyAt);
    // phase: 0 EXECUTED, 1 CANCELLED
    event MakerWithdrawResolved(uint64 indexed makerId, uint8 phase, address by, uint128 amount, address to);
    event DiscountReset(uint64 indexed makerId, address by);
}
```

| Call | Sent by | Does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| USDC `approve(vault, amount)`, then `depositMaker` | Any wallet | Pulls `amount` from the sender into your maker vault. `expectedController` is your maker wallet (`owner` in [Get maker capital](/hyperliquid/api-reference/makers/get-maker-capital)); any other reverts `ControllerMismatch()`. |
| `requestMakerWithdraw` | Your maker wallet | Starts the [delay](/hyperliquid/limits-and-fees) for `amount` to `to`. One can be pending at a time: a second reverts `WithdrawRequestLive()`. The request doesn't check your free cash; ask for at most the `MAKER_WITHDRAW` action in [Get maker capital](/hyperliquid/api-reference/makers/get-maker-capital). Execution re-checks your free collateral on-chain after the delay. |
| `executeMakerWithdraw` | Your maker wallet | Pays the pending request to its `to` once `readyAt` (Unix seconds) has passed. Reverts `WithdrawNotReady()` before then, and `InsufficientFree()` or `MakerShort()` if it would leave your positions uncovered. |
| `cancelMakerWithdraw` | Your maker wallet, or Totalis's guardian | Cancels the pending request. |
| `resetDiscount` | Your maker wallet, or Totalis's guardian | Sets your collateral discount to zero. |
| `makerOf`, `makerWithdrawals` | Anyone, as a view call | Your maker ID, and your pending request (`readyAt` is `0` when none is pending). |

A call from a wallet the table doesn't list reverts `Unauthorized()`. Every call that changes state
reverts `NoMaker()` for an unknown `makerId`; the view calls return zeros.
While the vault is paused, `depositMaker` and `executeMakerWithdraw` revert `Paused()`; the other calls
still work.

The [SDK](/hyperliquid/sdk#make-markets)'s `maker.controller()` builds each call for your wallet to
send.
