Any Totalis account can make markets. You need a maker account opened from your Totalis wallet, USDC
in its maker vault to back what you fill, a little HYPE for gas, a key for the maker, and a bot that
holds the [maker channel](/hyperliquid/api-reference/websocket/maker) open. You see every RFQ, quote the ones you
want, and earn the difference between what you quote and what the outcomes are worth.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Receive RFQs">
    Subscribe to the [maker channel](/hyperliquid/api-reference/websocket/maker). The snapshot holds every open RFQ,
    and `RFQ_CREATED` adds each new one: its legs, `stake` and `taker`. The RFQ carries no price.
    Price each leg from your own Hyperliquid market data.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Quote">
    [Create a quote](/hyperliquid/api-reference/quotes/create-quote) with the `payout` you will pay
    if every leg wins. It is unsigned and reserves nothing. Quote again to replace it, or
    [cancel it](/hyperliquid/api-reference/quotes/cancel-quote).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the fill">
    When a taker accepts, you get `QUOTE_ACCEPTED` with the exact terms and a `deadline` seconds
    away. Sign the terms and [confirm](/hyperliquid/api-reference/quotes/confirm-quote), or decline.
    Totalis puts the trade on-chain. A missed deadline fills nothing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Back the position">
    The taker's net stake moves into your maker vault and the position's worst case locks. Totalis
    settles it when the markets resolve: you keep the stake, or pay the winnings.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Endpoints

Every route below takes your quoting key, except Lower your collateral, which takes your collateral key.

| Step | Endpoint or event |
| --- | --- |
| Check your capital | [`GET /v1/makers/{maker_id}/capital`](/hyperliquid/api-reference/makers/get-maker-capital) |
| Receive RFQs | `RFQ_CREATED` on the [maker channel](/hyperliquid/api-reference/websocket/maker) |
| Read fees and the signing domain | [`GET /v1/deployment`](/hyperliquid/api-reference/deployment/get-deployment), no key |
| Quote, or replace your quote | [`POST /v1/rfqs/{rfq_id}/quotes`](/hyperliquid/api-reference/quotes/create-quote) |
| Pull a quote | [`DELETE /v1/quotes/{quote_id}`](/hyperliquid/api-reference/quotes/cancel-quote) |
| Pull every quote if your bot stops | [`PUT /v1/makers/{maker_id}/quote-lease`](/hyperliquid/api-reference/quotes/keep-quote-lease), [`DELETE`](/hyperliquid/api-reference/quotes/release-quote-lease) to release |
| Receive acceptances | `QUOTE_ACCEPTED` on the maker channel. Nothing is filled until you confirm. |
| Confirm or decline | [`POST /v1/quotes/{quote_id}/confirm`](/hyperliquid/api-reference/quotes/confirm-quote) |
| Reconcile after a restart | [`GET /v1/makers/{maker_id}/acceptances`](/hyperliquid/api-reference/quotes/list-acceptances) |
| Follow a fill on-chain | `OPERATION_UPDATED`, or [`GET /v1/me/operations`](/hyperliquid/api-reference/account/list-operations) |
| Read the positions you back | [`GET /v1/me/positions`](/hyperliquid/api-reference/positions/list-positions) |
| Read your fills and settlements | [`GET /v1/me/activity`](/hyperliquid/api-reference/account/list-activity) |
| Read your quotes and their outcomes | [`GET /v1/makers/{maker_id}/quote-history`](/hyperliquid/api-reference/makers/list-quote-history) |
| Lower your collateral | [`POST /v1/makers/{maker_id}/collateral-reductions`](/hyperliquid/guides/collateral#reduce-collateral) |
| Check a pending vault withdrawal | [`GET /v1/makers/{maker_id}/withdrawal`](/hyperliquid/api-reference/makers/get-maker-withdrawal) |

[/mm](https://hip4.totalis.trade/mm) shows your maker read-only: its onboarding state, balances, keys, a
pending withdrawal, positions, quotes and performance. It has no buttons that act. Its position, exposure
and performance pages read routes that only the app's session can call; a bot reads the same facts from the
routes above. Every write runs headless through the [SDK](/hyperliquid/sdk#make-markets) or the API:

| Credential | Signs or authenticates |
| --- | --- |
| Your wallet key | Sign-in, key approvals, every vault transaction, and the signature in every confirm |
| Your quoting key (`ttk_`) | Quotes, cancels, the quote lease, confirms and maker reads |
| Your collateral key (`ttk_`) | Collateral reductions and their self-funded transaction |

## Set up

You sign up once in a browser. Everything after that runs headless.

### In a browser, once

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get access">
    Totalis is invite-only for now. Email [founders@totalis.trade](mailto:founders@totalis.trade) for access and
    an invite code. Sign up at [hip4.totalis.trade](https://hip4.totalis.trade) with Google or email. Your
    account gets a wallet embedded in the app: that wallet is your maker wallet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Export the wallet key">
    In Account, under Private key, choose **Export private key**. Keep it only where your bot runs, as
    `TOTALIS_WALLET_KEY`. It signs your sign-ins, your keys' approvals, your vault transactions and every confirm.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
The wallet key controls your maker's funds. Anyone who has it can withdraw them. Never paste it into a website.
</Warning>

### Headless

Fund your wallet first (step 2). Then `examples/onboard.mjs` in the [SDK](/hyperliquid/sdk#examples) signs in, opens
and admits your maker, issues the quoting key, makes the first deposit and starts a bot. It does not issue the
collateral key. It prints the quoting key once: store it as `TOTALIS_MAKER_KEY`. Run it again with that set to resume:
each step skips what is already done, and it deposits only while the maker vault is empty.

```bash
TOTALIS_WALLET_KEY=0x... TOTALIS_DEPOSIT=1000000000 node examples/onboard.mjs
```

<Note>
On [staging](/hyperliquid/build/staging), sign up at [hip4-staging.totalis.trade](https://hip4-staging.totalis.trade)
with the staging invite code, and run with `TOTALIS_ENVIRONMENT=staging`. In the steps below, set `environment` to `"staging"`: the sign-in
then uses the staging app's origin, `https://hip4-staging.totalis.trade`.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Sign in with the wallet key and the Totalis app's origin, `https://hip4.totalis.trade`. Send the same origin
    as the `Origin` header with every request on the session.

    ```ts
    import { createClient, unwrap } from "@totalistrading/hip4-client";
    import { signInWithWallet } from "@totalistrading/hip4-client/wallet-session";
    import { privateKeyToAccount } from "viem/accounts";

    const wallet = privateKeyToAccount(process.env.TOTALIS_WALLET_KEY as `0x${string}`);
    const environment = "production"; // or "staging"
    const origin = environment === "staging" ? "https://hip4-staging.totalis.trade" : "https://hip4.totalis.trade";
    const session = await signInWithWallet({ environment, signer: wallet, origin });
    const onSession = { accessToken: session.access_token, headers: { Origin: origin } };
    const client = createClient(environment, onSession);
    const deployment = unwrap(await client.GET("/v1/deployment"));
    ```

    A session lasts at most 30 days and ends after 7 days unused
    ([Limits](/hyperliquid/limits-and-fees#wallet-sign-in-and-partners)). Then sign in again the same way. You need a
    session only to open and admit your maker and to manage its keys. Your bot runs on its maker key.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fund your wallet">
    Send HYPE for gas and the USDC you will quote with to your maker wallet on HyperEVM. Makers are never
    sponsored: your wallet pays the gas for every vault transaction.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open and admit your maker">
    Send `openMakerVaultTransaction(deployment, wallet.address)` from your wallet. A wallet opens one maker.
    `waitForMaker` waits until Totalis has indexed it, admits it ([Admit maker](/hyperliquid/api-reference/makers/admit-own-maker))
    and returns its `makerId`.

    `send` is your own: the SDK builds each `{ chain_id, to, data, value }` and never sends a transaction. With
    viem it is `walletClient.sendTransaction`, then waiting for the receipt.

    ```ts
    import { openMakerVaultTransaction, waitForMaker } from "@totalistrading/hip4-client/maker";

    await send(openMakerVaultTransaction(deployment, wallet.address)); // your wallet signs and sends it
    const readiness = await waitForMaker(client);
    if (readiness.state !== "READY") throw new Error(readiness.state); // see the table below
    const makerId = readiness.makerId;
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your keys">
    Issue the quoting bot's key, approved by your wallet's signature. Its scopes cannot move vault money.
    Copy the secret: it is shown once.

    ```ts
    import { COLLATERAL_SCOPES, createApiKeyClient, issueApiKey, QUOTING_BOT_SCOPES } from "@totalistrading/hip4-client/api-keys";
    import { toTotalisSigner } from "@totalistrading/hip4-client/signer";

    const keys = createApiKeyClient(environment, onSession);
    const owner = toTotalisSigner(wallet);
    const quoting = await issueApiKey(keys, owner, { maker: makerId, label: "Quoting bot", scopes: [...QUOTING_BOT_SCOPES], expires_in_days: 90 });
    // Only to lower your collateral; keep it apart from the quoting bot's.
    const collateral = await issueApiKey(keys, owner, { maker: makerId, label: "Collateral", scopes: [...COLLATERAL_SCOPES], expires_in_days: 90 });
    ```

    A key stops working at its expiry. Rotate it before then ([Manage keys](#manage-keys)), or pass
    `expires_in_days: 0` for a key that lasts until you revoke it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fund the maker vault">
    Approve USDC for the vault, then deposit it. Both come from your wallet.

    ```ts
    import { controllerTransactions } from "@totalistrading/hip4-client/maker";

    const vault = controllerTransactions(deployment, makerId, wallet.address);
    await send(vault.approveUsdc(1_000_000_000n)); // 1,000 USDC in atomic units
    await send(vault.deposit(1_000_000_000n));
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Quote">
    Run your bot on the quoting key with `createMaker`, signing confirms with your wallet key
    ([SDK](/hyperliquid/sdk#make-markets)). [Quote](#quote) and [Confirm](#confirm) below have the rules.
  </Step>
</Steps>

| `waitForMaker` returns | Do |
| --- | --- |
| `READY` | Create your keys and start quoting. |
| `PENDING` | Your maker is admitted and awaiting activation. Read again later. If it stays, email us. |
| `RESTRICTED` | It cannot quote. Email [founders@totalis.trade](mailto:founders@totalis.trade). |
| `WALLET_REQUIRED`, `OWNERSHIP_CONFLICT` | Your account has no single wallet that controls the maker. Email us before you open another maker. |

### Partner-admitted wallet

A [partner](/hyperliquid/build/overview) can run its maker from a wallet it admits
([Onboard a user](/hyperliquid/build/partner-frontend#onboard-a-user)) instead of a Totalis app account. There is no
browser step: admit the wallet, then follow the headless steps above, signing in with one of your registered
origins instead of the Totalis app's. With the example, call its exported `onboard` with that `origin`.

- Use a wallet of its own, not your builder signer. A wallet opens one maker.
- It signs in again whenever it needs a session, to manage its keys.
- It holds no vault balance of its own, like an app wallet that controls a maker
  ([Cash-outs](#cash-outs)).

## Manage keys

Your wallet manages your maker's keys from a wallet session ([Headless](#headless), step 1). An API key cannot create, rotate or
revoke keys. /mm lists them with their status, read-only.

| Do | SDK (`.../api-keys`) | API |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Create | `issueApiKey(keys, owner, { maker, label, scopes })` | [Create maker API key challenge](/hyperliquid/api-reference/api-keys/create-maker-api-key-challenge), then [Create maker API key](/hyperliquid/api-reference/api-keys/create-maker-api-key) |
| Rotate | `rotateApiKey(keys, owner, keyId, { maker, overlap_seconds })` | The challenge, then [Rotate maker API key](/hyperliquid/api-reference/api-keys/rotate-maker-api-key) |
| List | `listApiKeys(keys, { maker })` | [List maker API keys](/hyperliquid/api-reference/api-keys/list-maker-api-keys) |
| Revoke | `revokeApiKey(keys, keyId, { maker })` | [Revoke maker API key](/hyperliquid/api-reference/api-keys/revoke-maker-api-key) |

[Create, rotate, revoke](/hyperliquid/authentication#create-rotate-revoke) has the approval and the overlap rules,
and [Scopes](/hyperliquid/authentication#scopes) the two maker scope sets.

## Fund and withdraw

Your wallet sends every vault transaction and pays its gas in HYPE. `maker.controller()` builds each one on a
maker key, or `controllerTransactions(deployment, makerId, wallet.address)` without one.

| Do | Transaction | Then |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Deposit | `approveUsdc(amount)`, then `deposit(amount)` | `vault_gross` grows ([Get maker capital](/hyperliquid/api-reference/makers/get-maker-capital)). |
| Request a withdrawal | `requestWithdraw(amount, to)` | Ask for at most the `MAKER_WITHDRAW` action in your capital. It waits the [withdrawal delay](/hyperliquid/limits-and-fees), during which Totalis can cancel it. |
| Cancel it | `cancelWithdraw()` | Nothing moves. |
| Complete it | `executeWithdraw()` once `maker.withdrawal()` says it is executable | USDC goes to `to`. |
| Lower your collateral | `maker.reduceCollateral({ signTransaction })` on the collateral key | [Reduce collateral](/hyperliquid/guides/collateral#reduce-collateral) |

/mm shows a pending withdrawal and its countdown, read-only. [Move maker funds](/hyperliquid/guides/collateral#move-maker-funds)
has every vault call and its reverts.

## Your maker wallet

| Name | What it is | Where to read it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `maker_id` | Your maker's number, a decimal string. It names your maker key's account and your channel. | `waitForMaker`'s `makerId`, or the vault's `makerOf(wallet)` |
| `owner` | Your maker wallet. It opened the maker, funds, withdraws and approves keys. | [Get maker capital](/hyperliquid/api-reference/makers/get-maker-capital) |
| `signer` | The same wallet. Its signature confirms your fills. | Get maker capital |

Your maker is one wallet: your Totalis account's own wallet. Totalis admits a maker only from that wallet, so your
bot holds its key: the exported embedded wallet for an account created in the app, or your own key for a wallet a
partner admitted ([Partner-admitted wallet](#partner-admitted-wallet)). Nothing fills without your confirmation
signature, so to stop filling, stop confirming.

The vault cannot move a maker to another wallet.

- If the key leaks, email [founders@totalis.trade](mailto:founders@totalis.trade) at once. Totalis can cancel a
  pending maker withdrawal during its [delay](/hyperliquid/limits-and-fees). Then withdraw, and open a new maker
  from a new account.
- If the key is lost, the maker's funds cannot be withdrawn.
- Never call the vault's `setQuoteSigner`. A maker whose signer is not its own wallet stops being served.

## Price entries

Each market's sides carry a Hyperliquid `coin`, such as `#12090` for `YES` of outcome `1209`
([List markets](/hyperliquid/api-reference/markets/list-markets)). Read their books from Hyperliquid's info API
or WebSocket by that `coin`. Books update many times a second: keep the latest book per `coin` and price from
it, rather than processing every update.

Before you quote:

- Read [Get deployment](/hyperliquid/api-reference/deployment/get-deployment) for the current fees. A
  quote with other fees is refused.
- Read your capital. If `context.status` is not `CURRENT`, stop quoting until it is.

### Hedge on Hyperliquid

A Combo leg is a Hyperliquid outcome side, so you can hedge it on that outcome's HyperCore book.

| Combo leg | Hyperliquid |
| --- | --- |
| `side` | Side index: `0` for `YES`, `1` for `NO` |
| `outcome_id` and `side` | Encoding `10 * outcome_id + side` |
| Coin, for books and orders by name | `#` and the encoding: `YES` of outcome `1209` is `#12090` |
| Asset ID, for orders on the exchange endpoint | `100000000 + encoding`: `100012090` |
| Quote token | `quoteToken` in Hyperliquid's `outcomeMeta`. Every outcome on mainnet and testnet quotes in `USDC`. |

- Hyperliquid refuses an order worth under $10 (`Order must have minimum value of $10.`). Size hedges in
  steps of at least that, or net several Combos into one order.
- A leg and its outcome token settle on the same HyperCore result. Hyperliquid pays `settleFraction` per
  `YES` token and `1 - settleFraction` per `NO` token, and the Combo leg's result is the same fraction
  ([Settlement](/hyperliquid/guides/trading#settlement)).

Hyperliquid documents the encoding on [Asset IDs](https://hyperliquid.gitbook.io/hyperliquid-docs/for-developers/api/asset-ids)
and settlement on [HIP-4](https://hyperliquid.gitbook.io/hyperliquid-docs/hyperliquid-improvement-proposals-hips/hip-4-outcome-markets).

## Quote

```bash
curl https://hip4-api.totalis.trade/v1/rfqs/$RFQ_ID/quotes \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $TOTALIS_MAKER_KEY" \
  -H "Idempotency-Key: 01a0df4e-5f11-7a2b-9c3d-4e5f6a7b8c9d" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "quote_id": "0x5e5e...5e5e",
    "entry": {
      "maker_id": "7",
      "legs": [{ "outcome_id": "1209", "side": "YES" }],
      "stake": "25000000",
      "payout": "98500000",
      "fee_bps": 100,
      "taker_fee_bps": 150,
      "taker": "0x1111111111111111111111111111111111111111",
      "expiry": "1790452875",
      "salt": "48151623429108"
    }
  }'
```

```json
{
  "quote_id": "0x5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e",
  "accept_before": "2026-09-26T20:01:01Z"
}
```

| Field | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| `legs`, `stake`, `taker` | Copied from the RFQ, unchanged. |
| `maker_id` | Your maker. |
| `payout` | What you pay if every leg wins, gross of the profit fee. More than `stake`, and at most the maximum payout on [Limits](/hyperliquid/limits-and-fees). |
| `fee_bps`, `taker_fee_bps` | Exactly the current values in [Get deployment](/hyperliquid/api-reference/deployment/get-deployment). |
| `expiry` | Unix seconds, more than 14 and at most 30 seconds ahead ([Limits](/hyperliquid/limits-and-fees)). Takers can accept until the response's `accept_before`, 14 seconds before it. |
| `salt` | A random uint256. |
| `quote_id` | The EIP-712 digest of `entry` as `Quote` ([Signing](/hyperliquid/authentication#types)). It becomes the `position_id`. |

An entry quote that could never fill is refused when you send it: `400 INVALID_REQUEST`, with
`field_violations` naming the field.

| `field_violations` | Why |
| --- | --- |
| `/quote_id` | Not the EIP-712 digest of `entry` as `Quote`. |
| `/entry/maker_id` | Not your key's maker. |
| `/entry/legs`, `/entry/stake`, `/entry/taker` | Differ from the RFQ. |
| `/entry/stake` | The RFQ's net stake is under the minimum. |
| `/entry/payout` | Not above `stake`, or over the maximum payout. |
| `/entry/fee_bps`, `/entry/taker_fee_bps` | Differ from Get deployment now. |
| `/entry/expiry` | 14 seconds or less ahead, or more than 30. |

A leg that has already settled returns `409 RFQ_NOT_OPEN`. The minimum, the maximum payout and the window
are on [Limits](/hyperliquid/limits-and-fees).

### Quoting rules

- Price the net stake. The taker fee comes off `stake` before it reaches you, so you risk
  `payout - net stake`.
- Your newest quote on an RFQ replaces your last one. The taker sees `QUOTE_REMOVED` for the old one.
- An entry quote can be accepted until its `accept_before`. Quote again before it passes to stay on the RFQ. The
  RFQ stops taking new quotes at `expires_at`, but a live quote stays acceptable until its `accept_before`. Neither
  sends an event. The windows are on [Limits](/hyperliquid/limits-and-fees).
- A quote reserves no collateral. Capacity is checked when you confirm, so quote only what
  `free_after_pending` covers ([When collateral is checked](/hyperliquid/guides/collateral#when-collateral-is-checked)).
- A `409 RFQ_NOT_OPEN` means the RFQ closed, a newer one replaced it, or one of its legs settled. Drop it.
- While a taker's acceptance of your live quote on an RFQ waits for your answer and its `deadline` has not
  passed, a new quote on that RFQ (entry or cash-out) returns `409 STATE_CONFLICT`. Nothing changes: the
  acceptance still waits for you. Confirm or decline it first, or wait for its `deadline`. Past the `deadline`
  it no longer blocks, and your new quote marks it `TIMED_OUT`.
- An exact retry of a quote that already went through returns the original `quote_id`.

### Cancel a quote

```bash
curl -X DELETE https://hip4-api.totalis.trade/v1/quotes/$QUOTE_ID \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $TOTALIS_MAKER_KEY"
```

```json
{ "status": "CANCELLED" }
```

`CANCELLED` or `REPLACED` means it is off the RFQ. A `409` with `{ "status": "ACCEPTED" }` means you already
confirmed it: it is filling, and cannot be cancelled.

While a taker's acceptance of the quote waits for your answer, cancel returns `409 STATE_CONFLICT` (an error
body) and changes nothing. To refuse the fill, [confirm](#confirm) with `{ "decision": "DECLINE" }`. Once the
acceptance is answered or past its `deadline`, the cancel goes through, so repeating it is safe. Past the
`deadline`, cancelling marks the acceptance `TIMED_OUT`.

### Quote lease

A lease pulls your quotes if your bot goes quiet, so takers don't accept quotes no one will confirm. Arm it,
and renew it well inside `lease_ms`, for example every third of it:

```bash
curl -X PUT https://hip4-api.totalis.trade/v1/makers/$MAKER_ID/quote-lease \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $TOTALIS_MAKER_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{ "lease_ms": 15000 }'
```

```json
{ "lease_ms": 15000, "expires_at": "2026-09-26T20:00:21.000Z" }
```

If a renewal does not arrive before `expires_at`, Totalis cancels each of your open quotes no taker is
accepting, and every quote you publish after, until you renew or release it. The lease covers the whole maker,
whichever key renews it. It takes no `Idempotency-Key`. Release it with `DELETE` on the same path. The range
is on [Limits](/hyperliquid/limits-and-fees).

## Confirm

`QUOTE_ACCEPTED` carries the exact terms, named by `kind`. Check them against what you quoted, and check your
price and your capital still hold. Then sign them (`entry` as `Quote`, `sell_back` as `SellBack`, `transfer`
as `CashoutTransfer`) and answer with no `Idempotency-Key`:

```bash
curl https://hip4-api.totalis.trade/v1/quotes/$QUOTE_ID/confirm \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $TOTALIS_MAKER_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{ "decision": "CONFIRM", "maker_signature": "0xcdcd...cd1c" }'
```

```json
{
  "quote_id": "0x5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e",
  "rfq_id": "01a0df4e-5e07-7a3c-8f21-d4e6b7a90c1a",
  "status": "ACCEPTED",
  "deadline": "2026-09-26T20:00:53.352Z",
  "operation_id": "0x9a9a9a9a9a9a9a9a9a9a9a9a9a9a9a9a9a9a9a9a9a9a9a9a9a9a9a9a9a9a9a9a"
}
```

| `status` | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `ACCEPTED` | Queued as `operation_id`, not yet on-chain. |
| `FILLED` | The trade landed. `POSITION_OPENED` follows an entry, `POSITION_CLOSED` a `sell_back`, and `POSITION_TRANSFERRED` a `transfer`. |
| `FAILED` | The trade reverted on-chain, or expired before it was sent. Nothing filled. |
| `DECLINED`, `TIMED_OUT`, `CANCELLED` | Nothing filled. |

Follow `operation_id` on `OPERATION_UPDATED`. `COMMITTED` means filled. `REVERTED` or
`EXPIRED_UNEXECUTED` means nothing filled: the contract checks your capital and the terms again when the trade
lands, and an entry also expires unsent when the taker's funding does not arrive in time. Every confirm is checked against your capacity first
([When collateral is checked](/hyperliquid/guides/collateral#when-collateral-is-checked)). On an RFQ without
`intent_id`, that check is skipped while Totalis's view of the chain lags, and the vault decides, so an `ACCEPTED`
fill can still revert. Keep following `OPERATION_UPDATED`.

- `{ "decision": "DECLINE" }` needs no signature. Decline when your price or your capital moved.
- The confirm deadline is on [Limits](/hyperliquid/limits-and-fees). It is short, so sign on a hot
  path: keep the deployment and your signer loaded, and answer before any slow work.
- A lost response: resend the same answer. Until you know the outcome, treat the quote as filled.

| Error | Means | Do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `409 QUOTE_CAPACITY_EXCEEDED` | Your free capital, less your confirmed fills not yet on-chain, does not cover this fill. For a `transfer`, your wallet's own vault balance does not cover its `price`. | Nothing filled. Quote smaller, or add capital. |
| `409 QUOTE_EXPIRED`, `QUOTE_SIGNER_CHANGED`, `RFQ_NOT_OPEN` | The quote or the RFQ moved on. | Nothing filled. Drop it. |
| `409 RFQ_GENERATION_STALE` | RFQs with `intent_id` only: a newer RFQ replaced this one. | Nothing filled. Drop it. |
| `409 STATE_CONFLICT` | The deadline passed, or the trade can no longer open, for example because a leg resolved. | Nothing filled. |
| `422 INVALID_SIGNATURE`, `WRONG_AUTHORITY` | The signature is not your maker wallet's, over these exact terms. | Fix the signer, and resend before the deadline. |
| `429 QUOTE_EXPOSURE_LIMIT` | RFQs with `intent_id` only: too many fills are pending for your maker, or on this RFQ. | Nothing filled. |
| `503 QUOTE_EXPOSURE_STALE`, `COMBO_AUTOMATIC_DISABLED` | RFQs with `intent_id` only: Totalis cannot check this fill right now. | Resend the same answer before the deadline. |
| `503 DEPENDENCY_STALE` | Totalis cannot check this fill right now. | Resend the same answer before the deadline. |

Automatic RFQs carry `intent_id`: every RFQ the Totalis app opens, and any RFQ a taker opens with one
([Maker channel](/hyperliquid/api-reference/websocket/maker#event)). Every code is on
[Errors](/hyperliquid/errors#codes).

### After a restart

Subscribe from your saved cursor. On `REPLAY`, the replayed `QUOTE_ACCEPTED` events carry what arrived while you
were away. On `SNAPSHOT` or `RESET`, the snapshot's `acceptances` holds every acceptance still waiting for you,
earliest deadline first. Either way, [List acceptances](/hyperliquid/api-reference/quotes/list-acceptances) returns
every one still waiting. Answer each
one before its `deadline`, and don't quote those RFQs again first. Keep the `quote_id`s you created in durable
storage, so a restarted bot confirms only terms it published.

## Profit and loss

A position pays `effective = payout * r1 * r2 * ...`, rounded down, where each `r` is a leg's result: `1` for a
win, `0` for a loss, or a fraction ([Settlement](/hyperliquid/guides/trading#settlement)).

| Outcome | You |
| --- | --- |
| Any leg loses | Keep the taker's net stake. |
| Every leg wins | Pay `payout - net stake`. |
| A leg resolves to a fraction | Pay `effective - net stake` if `effective` is at least the net stake (`WIN`). Otherwise keep `net stake - effective` (`PARTIAL`). |
| You buy it back | Pay the `sell_back` price, and the net stake you held is freed. Net, you pay `price - net stake`. The position closes. |
| Another maker buys it | Nothing. You still back it, for the new owner. |

The profit fee comes out of the owner's winnings, and a sell-back's fee out of the owner's proceeds
([Cash out](/hyperliquid/guides/trading#cash-out)). Neither comes from your vault.

Read the positions you back with [List positions](/hyperliquid/api-reference/positions/list-positions)
and your fills and settlements with [List activity](/hyperliquid/api-reference/account/list-activity), both with
your maker key. `settlement_kind` and each leg's result are on
[Trading](/hyperliquid/guides/trading#settlement).

## Collateral

Each fill locks the position's worst case in your maker vault: `payout - net stake`. Positions that
cannot all win together can share that collateral once you run a collateral reduction.
[Maker collateral](/hyperliquid/guides/collateral) has every capital field, how each step moves it, and how
to reduce it.

## Cash-outs

A taker can sell an open position before it settles. Totalis opens a cash-out RFQ to every maker:
`RFQ_CREATED` with `kind: CASHOUT`, the `position_id`, its `owner`, the backing `maker_id`, its `net_stake`
and `payout`, and the leg state to price against.

| You | Quote | You pay from | The position |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Back the position (`maker_id` is yours) | `sell_back` terms, a `price` | Your maker vault | Closes |
| Do not back it | `transfer` terms, a `price` | Your maker wallet's own vault balance, not the maker vault | Moves to your wallet's account. Its original maker still backs it. |

Bid only on positions you back: your maker wallet cannot hold its own vault balance.
[Create deposit](/hyperliquid/api-reference/deposits/create-deposit) refuses a wallet that controls a maker, and
Totalis moves an [embedded wallet's](/hyperliquid/glossary) free vault cash to HyperCore in the background. A
`transfer` your wallet's free vault balance cannot cover is refused when you publish it (`400 INVALID_REQUEST`
naming `transfer.price`). While Totalis's view of the vault lags the chain, publishing skips that check;
confirm then answers `QUOTE_CAPACITY_EXCEEDED` once the view is current, or the vault refuses the transfer
on-chain. A position you buy by
transfer belongs to your wallet's account, not your maker: read it and its settlement with a key for
[your account](/hyperliquid/authentication#keys).

```bash
curl https://hip4-api.totalis.trade/v1/rfqs/$RFQ_ID/quotes \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $TOTALIS_MAKER_KEY" \
  -H "Idempotency-Key: 01a0df4e-6a20-7c11-8d4e-5f6a7b8c9d0e" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "quote_id": "0x7c7c...7c7c",
    "sell_back": {
      "position_id": "0x5e5e...5e5e",
      "price": "18000000",
      "owner": "0x1111111111111111111111111111111111111111",
      "expiry": "1790452915",
      "salt": "93841120577316",
      "leg_state": "0x3d3d...3d3d"
    }
  }'
```

`position_id`, `owner` and `leg_state` are copied from the RFQ. `quote_id` is the EIP-712 digest of
`sell_back` as `SellBack`. A `transfer` body has `seller` (the owner) and `buyer` (your maker wallet) in place
of `owner`, and is signed as `CashoutTransfer`. The `expiry` window is on [Limits](/hyperliquid/limits-and-fees).

### Price a cash-out

| RFQ field | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `legs`, `legs_hash` | The position's legs. `legs_hash` is the EIP-712 hash of `legs` as `Leg[]`: check it matches before you price. |
| `leg_state` | Each leg's on-chain state when Totalis opened the RFQ, hashed. Sign it unchanged. |
| `decision_block`, `decision_read_gas` | The HyperEVM block Totalis read `leg_state` at, and the read gas it used. |
| `net_stake`, `payout` | What the position holds and pays if every leg wins. |

`leg_state` is `keccak256(abi.encode(states))`, with one `uint256` per leg in order:
`(code << 64) | value`. `code` is `0` for an open leg, `1` for a resolved leg and `2` for a pruned leg.
For a resolved leg, `value` is the outcome's resolved value out of `100000000`, the same for either side: a
`YES` leg's result is `value / 100000000`, and a `NO` leg's is `(100000000 - value) / 100000000`. It is `0`
otherwise. To check it, call the vault's view `legStateOf(Leg[] legs, uint256 readGas)` with `legs` and
`decision_read_gas` at `decision_block`.

- Price from current books, not from `decision_block`. Drop legs already resolved as wins.
- A position with a lost leg is worth nothing. Bid on a leg resolved to a fraction, or pruned, only if you can
  value it.
- The sale lands only while every leg's state still equals `leg_state`. If a leg moves first, the trade
  reverts and nothing moves.

## Compatibility

RFQs, acceptances, capital and events gain fields and enum values as the API grows. A bot that rejects an
unknown field stops quoting the day one ships. Ignore fields you don't know, and treat an unknown enum value as
a state you can't act on ([Compatibility](/hyperliquid/api-reference/introduction#compatibility)).
