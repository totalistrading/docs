Any Totalis account can make markets. You need a maker account opened from your Totalis wallet, USDC
in its maker vault to back what you fill, a little HYPE for gas, a key for the maker, and a bot that
holds the [maker channel](/hyperliquid/api-reference/websocket/maker) open. You see every RFQ, quote the ones you
want, and earn the difference between what you quote and what the outcomes are worth.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Receive RFQs">
    Subscribe to the [maker channel](/hyperliquid/api-reference/websocket/maker). The snapshot holds every open RFQ,
    and `RFQ_CREATED` adds each new one: its legs, `stake` and `taker`. The RFQ carries no price.
    Price each leg from your own Hyperliquid market data.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Quote">
    [Create a quote](/hyperliquid/api-reference/quotes/create-quote) with the `payout` you will pay
    if every leg wins. It is unsigned and reserves nothing. Quote again to replace it, or
    [cancel it](/hyperliquid/api-reference/quotes/cancel-quote).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the fill">
    When a taker accepts, you get `QUOTE_ACCEPTED` with the exact terms and a `deadline` seconds
    away. Sign the terms and [confirm](/hyperliquid/api-reference/quotes/confirm-quote), or decline.
    Totalis puts the trade on-chain. A missed deadline fills nothing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Back the position">
    The taker's net stake moves into your maker vault and the position's worst case locks. Totalis
    settles it when the markets resolve: you keep the stake, or pay the winnings.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Endpoints

| Step | Endpoint or event |
| --- | --- |
| Check your capital | [`GET /v1/makers/{maker_id}/capital`](/hyperliquid/api-reference/makers/get-maker-capital) |
| Receive RFQs | `RFQ_CREATED` on the [maker channel](/hyperliquid/api-reference/websocket/maker) |
| Read fees and the signing domain | [`GET /v1/deployment`](/hyperliquid/api-reference/deployment/get-deployment) |
| Quote, or replace your quote | [`POST /v1/rfqs/{rfq_id}/quotes`](/hyperliquid/api-reference/quotes/create-quote) |
| Pull a quote | [`DELETE /v1/quotes/{quote_id}`](/hyperliquid/api-reference/quotes/cancel-quote) |
| Receive acceptances | `QUOTE_ACCEPTED` on the maker channel. Nothing is filled until you confirm. |
| Confirm or decline | [`POST /v1/quotes/{quote_id}/confirm`](/hyperliquid/api-reference/quotes/confirm-quote) |
| Reconcile after a restart | [`GET /v1/makers/{maker_id}/acceptances`](/hyperliquid/api-reference/quotes/list-acceptances) |
| Read the positions you back | [`GET /v1/me/positions`](/hyperliquid/api-reference/positions/list-positions) with your maker key |
| Lower your collateral | [`POST /v1/makers/{maker_id}/collateral-reductions`](/hyperliquid/guides/collateral#reduce-collateral) |

## Set up

At [hip4.totalis.trade/mm](https://hip4.totalis.trade/mm):

1. Under **Your wallet**, add a little HYPE for gas and the USDC you will quote with.
2. Choose **Create maker account**: one transaction from your wallet. Your `maker_id` is assigned
   then. A wallet opens one maker.
3. Choose **Fund vault** to move USDC into the maker vault.
4. Create a key for the maker with the [maker scopes](/hyperliquid/authentication#scopes).
5. Run your bot with that key, your `maker_id` and your [wallet key](/hyperliquid/authentication#wallet-key).

Makers are never sponsored. Your wallet pays the gas for every maker vault call, in HYPE.

## Quote

```bash
curl https://hip4-api.totalis.trade/v1/rfqs/$RFQ_ID/quotes \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $TOTALIS_MAKER_KEY" \
  -H "Idempotency-Key: 01a0df4e-5f11-7a2b-9c3d-4e5f6a7b8c9d" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "quote_id": "0x5e5e...5e5e",
    "entry": {
      "maker_id": "7",
      "legs": [{ "outcome_id": "1209", "side": "YES" }],
      "stake": "25000000",
      "payout": "98500000",
      "fee_bps": 100,
      "taker_fee_bps": 150,
      "taker": "0x1111111111111111111111111111111111111111",
      "expiry": "1790452875",
      "salt": "48151623429108"
    }
  }'
```

```json
{ "quote_id": "0x5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e" }
```

| Field | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| `legs`, `stake`, `taker` | Copied from the RFQ. |
| `payout` | What you pay if every leg wins, gross of the profit fee. |
| `fee_bps`, `taker_fee_bps` | The current values in [Get deployment](/hyperliquid/api-reference/deployment/get-deployment). |
| `expiry` | Unix seconds. |
| `salt` | A random uint256. |
| `quote_id` | The EIP-712 digest of `entry` as `Quote` ([Signing](/hyperliquid/authentication#types)). It becomes the `position_id`. |

### Quoting rules

- Price the net stake. The taker fee comes off `stake` before it reaches you, so you risk
  `payout - net stake`.
- Your newest quote on an RFQ replaces your last one. The taker sees `QUOTE_REMOVED` for the old one.
- A quote leaves the RFQ at its `expiry`, and the RFQ closes at `expires_at`. Neither sends an event.
  The windows, and how close to `expiry` a quote stops being shown, are on [Limits](/hyperliquid/limits-and-fees).
- A quote reserves no collateral. Capacity is checked when you confirm, so quote only what
  `free_after_pending` covers ([When collateral is checked](/hyperliquid/guides/collateral#when-collateral-is-checked)).
- A `409` with `RFQ_NOT_OPEN` or `RFQ_GENERATION_STALE` means the RFQ moved on. Drop it, or
  re-read it and quote again.

### Cancel a quote

```bash
curl -X DELETE https://hip4-api.totalis.trade/v1/quotes/$QUOTE_ID \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $TOTALIS_MAKER_KEY"
```

```json
{ "status": "CANCELLED" }
```

`CANCELLED` or `REPLACED` means it is off the RFQ. `409` with `ACCEPTED` means a taker already
accepted it: answer the acceptance.

## Confirm

`QUOTE_ACCEPTED` carries the exact terms, named by `kind`. Check them against what you quoted, then
sign them (`entry` as `Quote`, `sell_back` as `SellBack`, `transfer` as `CashoutTransfer`) and answer
with no `Idempotency-Key`:

```bash
curl https://hip4-api.totalis.trade/v1/quotes/$QUOTE_ID/confirm \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $TOTALIS_MAKER_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{ "decision": "CONFIRM", "maker_signature": "0xcdcd...cd1c" }'
```

```json
{
  "quote_id": "0x5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e5e",
  "rfq_id": "01a0df4e-5e07-7a3c-8f21-d4e6b7a90c1a",
  "status": "ACCEPTED",
  "deadline": "2026-09-26T20:00:53.352Z",
  "operation_id": "0x9a9a9a9a9a9a9a9a9a9a9a9a9a9a9a9a9a9a9a9a9a9a9a9a9a9a9a9a9a9a9a9a"
}
```

| `status` | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `ACCEPTED` | Queued as `operation_id`. `POSITION_OPENED` follows when it lands. |
| `DECLINED`, `TIMED_OUT`, `CANCELLED` | Nothing filled. |

- `{ "decision": "DECLINE" }` needs no signature. Decline when your price or your capital moved.
- The confirm deadline is on [Limits](/hyperliquid/limits-and-fees). It is short, so sign on a hot
  path: keep the deployment and your signer loaded, and answer before any slow work.
- A lost response: resend the same answer. Until you know the outcome, treat the quote as filled.
- `QUOTE_CAPACITY_EXCEEDED` means your capital cannot cover this fill now. Nothing filled.

### After a restart

Subscribe from your saved cursor. The maker snapshot's `acceptances` holds every acceptance still
waiting for you, earliest deadline first, and
[List acceptances](/hyperliquid/api-reference/quotes/list-acceptances) pages the rest. Answer each
one before its `deadline`. Keep the `quote_id`s you created in durable storage, so a restarted bot
confirms only terms it published.

## Profit and loss

| Outcome | You |
| --- | --- |
| Any leg loses | Keep the taker's net stake |
| Every leg wins | Pay `payout - net stake`. The profit fee comes out of the taker's winnings, not your vault. |
| A leg pays part (a `PARTIAL` result) | Keep the part of the net stake the position does not pay back |
| You buy it back | Pay the `sell_back` price. The position closes. |
| Another maker buys it | Nothing. You still back it, for the new owner. |

Read the positions you back with [List positions](/hyperliquid/api-reference/positions/list-positions)
and your maker key. `settlement_kind` and each leg's result are on
[Trading](/hyperliquid/guides/trading#settlement).

## Collateral

Each fill locks the position's worst case in your maker vault: `payout - net stake`. Positions that
cannot all win together can share that collateral once you run a collateral reduction.
[Maker collateral](/hyperliquid/guides/collateral) has every capital field, how each step moves it, and how
to reduce it.

## Cash-outs

A taker can sell an open position before it settles. Totalis opens a cash-out RFQ to every maker:
`RFQ_CREATED` with `kind: CASHOUT`, the `position_id`, its `owner` and the backing `maker_id`.

| You | Quote | You pay from | The position |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Back the position | `sell_back` terms, a `price` | Your maker vault | Closes |
| Do not back it | `transfer` terms, a `price` | Your own vault balance, not the maker vault | Moves to you. Its original maker still backs it. |

Price the position at the RFQ's `decision_block` and sign its `leg_state` unchanged. Keep your own
vault balance at zero to sell back only. A cash-out quote's `expiry` window is on
[Limits](/hyperliquid/limits-and-fees).

## Your identity

| Name | What it is | Where to read it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `maker_id` | Your maker's number, a decimal string. It names your maker key's account and your channel. | The maker page in the app |
| `owner` | Your maker wallet. It opened the maker, funds, withdraws and approves keys. | [Get maker capital](/hyperliquid/api-reference/makers/get-maker-capital) |
| `signer` | The same wallet. Its signature confirms your fills. | Get maker capital |

Your maker is one wallet, so `owner` and `signer` are the same address. Run your bot with that wallet's
key. Nothing fills without your confirmation signature, so to stop filling, stop confirming.
