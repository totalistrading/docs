Follow your [position](/hyperliquid/api-reference/positions/get-position) until it closes. Totalis submits
settlement and claims automatically. A market result, a settled position, a vault credit and a withdrawal are
different events.

## Calculate the return

Each leg's `result_numerator / result_denominator` is the payout factor for the side you selected.
A YES factor of `0.25` means the NO factor is `0.75`. A fraction is a payout rule, not an instruction to
refund the stake.

Use integers throughout. With gross stake `G`, entry fee rate `t`, position profit fee rate `b` and quoted
maximum payout `P`:

```text
entry_fee = floor(G * t / 10000)
net_stake = G - entry_fee
effective_payout = floor(P * product(result_numerator) / product(result_denominator))
profit = max(effective_payout - net_stake, 0)
profit_fee = floor(profit * b / 10000)
total_return_after_all_claims = effective_payout - profit_fee
trading_profit = total_return_after_all_claims - G
```

Multiply the factors before the final division. Rounding after each leg can change the return.
The position keeps its original `fee_bps`. Current fee settings come from
[Get deployment](/hyperliquid/api-reference/deployment/get-deployment); fee rules live on
[Limits and fees](/hyperliquid/limits-and-fees).

## Worked returns

These illustrative terms use a 10 USDC gross stake, a 20 bps entry fee, a 100 USDC maximum payout and a
100 bps profit fee. They are examples, not current fee quotes. The entry fee is 0.02 USDC and the net stake
is 9.98 USDC. Amounts below are USDC after all available profit claims finish. All unspecified legs are
fully resolved at 1; the last row explicitly leaves another leg unresolved.

| Selected-side factors | Effective payout | `settlement_kind` | Profit fee | Total returned | Profit against gross stake |
| --- | ---: | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Every leg is 1 | 100 | `WIN` | 0.9002 | 99.0998 | 89.0998 |
| One resolved leg is 0 | 0 | `LOSS` | 0 | 0 | -10 |
| One leg is 0.05 | 5 | `PARTIAL` | 0 | 5 | -5 |
| One leg is 0.0998 | 9.98 | `WIN` | 0 | 9.98 | -0.02 |
| Two legs are 0.25 and 0.5 | 12.5 | `WIN` | 0.0252 | 12.4748 | 2.4748 |
| Two legs are 0.25 and 0.25 | 6.25 | `PARTIAL` | 0 | 6.25 | -3.75 |
| One leg is 0 and another is unresolved | 0 | `EARLY_LOST` | 0 | 0 | -10 |

`WIN` means the effective payout reaches the net stake. It does not necessarily mean all legs paid in full
or that you recovered the entry fee. Positive factors can also round to a zero payout.

An early loss is possible whenever the payout rounds to zero even if every unresolved leg later pays in full.
Otherwise an unresolved leg keeps the position open.

## Settlement and payment

| Result | Vault credit at settlement | What follows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Zero payout | Nothing. | The position closes. |
| Positive payout below net stake | The effective payout. | The position closes with `PARTIAL`. |
| Payout equal to net stake | The net stake. | The position closes with `WIN` and no profit to claim. |
| Payout above net stake | The net stake. | Gross profit remains in `unclaimed_profit`; Totalis claims it into your vault, less the profit fee. |

Profit claims depend on distributable funds in the backing maker's vault. A claim can pay part of the profit
or wait for more funds. `SETTLED_UNCLAIMED` can therefore persist; elapsed time does not establish payment.
`unclaimed_profit` is the gross amount still outstanding, not the full historical payout.
The fee calculation across partial claims gives the same total fee as a full claim. Inspect the position,
activity and current balances for actual credits.

For an [embedded wallet](/hyperliquid/guides/funding#where-your-cash-sits), a later sweep can move free vault
cash to HyperCore. A vault credit is not a withdrawal to an external address.

## Exceptional results

| Observation | Settlement behavior | Your integration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A terminal numeric result | The contract uses the selected-side factor and can preserve it in its settlement cache. | Use the observed factors; never derive a substitute from the title or current price. |
| A fractional numeric result, including a tie | The fraction participates in the same product. | Show the fractional return and fees. Do not label it an automatic stake refund. |
| A recognised nonnumeric result without a cached terminal factor | The leg remains unresolved for grading. | Keep the obligation pending unless an early loss already determines a zero payout. |
| A result read fails or providers disagree | An unavailable read does not establish a result. | Keep the last known state and retry reads with backoff. |
| HyperCore prunes a result already cached by the vault | The cached terminal factor remains usable. | Follow the position's on-chain settlement. |
| A permanently pruned result was never cached | There is no general refund or result-reconstruction action in this vault generation. Settlement can remain blocked indefinitely unless an early loss decides it. | Escalate with the position ID and outcome IDs. An eligible signed cash-out is a separate trade and needs a willing buyer. |
| An event is postponed, cancelled, or past its displayed date | The event label or date alone does not settle the position. The market's authoritative factor controls. | Read the market's published rules and actual result; do not invent a factor or time-based refund. |
| Historical amount fields are unavailable | Missing display evidence does not itself cancel the position or prove a zero credit. | Preserve unknown values and reconcile the position, operations and account activity. |

Cached terminal factors are write-once in this generation. A later native change does not rewrite a factor
already cached by the vault. The current contracts have no general dispute or time-based refund action.

## Cash-out comparison

A cash-out is a separate signed sale before settlement. With the example's 9.98 USDC net stake and 100 bps
profit fee:

| Bid | Gross price | Fee | Cash received |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| `SELL_BACK` to the backing maker | 12.5 | 0.0252 | 12.4748 |
| `SELL_BACK` below net stake | 5 | 0 | 5 |
| An executable `TRANSFER` | 12.5 | 0 | 12.5 |

Compare `netCashout(bid, position)` when bids have different kinds. A higher gross price can return less
after fees. A transfer changes ownership; the new owner's later profit fee still uses the position's original
net stake and profit fee rate. Buyer availability is described under
[Maker cash-outs](/hyperliquid/guides/market-making#cash-outs).

## Track a delayed position

| State | Action |
| --- | --- |
| `OPEN` | Inspect unresolved legs. Request a cash-out only if you want to sell and a bid is available. |
| `AWAITING_SETTLEMENT` | Follow position and operation updates. A deciding result does not itself prove a vault credit. |
| `SETTLED_UNCLAIMED` | Follow remaining profit and balance updates. Do not count gross outstanding profit as free cash. |
| `CLOSED` | Read `close_reason`, `settlement_kind`, `settlement_payout` and activity to identify the outcome. |

For a persistent delay, email [founders@totalis.trade](mailto:founders@totalis.trade) with the environment,
position ID, deciding outcome IDs, operation IDs and any transaction hashes. Keep credentials and signed
authorizations out of support messages.
