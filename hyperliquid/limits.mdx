Every other Hyperliquid page links here for these values. Vault limits are set on the vault contract,
where they can change. The current fees are in
[Get deployment](/hyperliquid/api-reference/deployment/get-deployment).

## Trading

| Limit | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Legs per RFQ | 1 to 50, in strictly ascending `outcome_id` order, no outcome twice |
| Minimum stake | Net stake (stake minus the entry fee) of at least the vault's `minStake`, currently 1 USDC |
| Maximum position payout | 100,000 USDC |
| Fees | `taker_fee_bps` (entry fee, on the stake, at accept) and `fee_bps` (profit fee, on winnings, at claim), currently 20 and 100. Every quote must carry the current values. |
| Entry RFQ open for quotes | 20 seconds after creation |
| Cash-out RFQ open for quotes | 30 seconds after creation |
| Entry quote `expiry` | In the future and at most 30 seconds after creation. Quotes within 2 seconds of expiry are left out of the RFQ's quotes. |
| Cash-out quote `expiry` | 25 to 60 seconds after creation, so the sale can still execute after the confirm. Quotes within 22 seconds of expiry are left out. |
| Confirm deadline | 2 seconds after the accept. The accept response arrives at most 1 second after it. |
| Settlement and claim | Totalis runs both for every eligible position about every 5 minutes. |

## Signing windows

| Signature | Window |
| --- | --- |
| Accept funding (USDC `ReceiveWithAuthorization`) | At most 900 seconds from `valid_after` to `valid_before`, and `valid_before` at least 5 seconds ahead |
| Withdrawal vault part (`Exit`) | `expiry` at least 5 and at most 3,600 seconds ahead, less a 5 second margin |
| Withdrawal payout (USDC `TransferWithAuthorization`) | At most 4,500 seconds from `valid_after` to `valid_before`, and `valid_before` at least 300 seconds after the `Exit` `expiry` (or after now, with no `Exit`) |
| Maker vault withdrawal delay | 86,400 seconds between `requestMakerWithdraw` and `executeMakerWithdraw` |

## Streams

| Limit | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Replay window | 24 hours. An older cursor resets to a snapshot. |
| Snapshot | At most 100 items per class. The rest is in the route named in `truncated`. |
| Heartbeat | 15 seconds, or 1 second with `publication_freshness` |
| Market data stream | 1 to 256 outcomes per subscription, frames up to 1 MiB |

## Keys and requests

| Limit | Value |
| --- | --- |
| API key lifetime | 1 to 90 days, or Forever |
| Live API keys | 20 per account, counting keys in a rotation overlap |
| Key approval message | Valid for 5 minutes, usable once |
| Request body | 64 KiB |

## Rate limits

A request over its quota returns `429 RATE_LIMITED` with a `Retry-After` header holding the seconds to
wait. Wait at least that long before resending. The SDK does this for you.

| Route | Per account, per minute |
| --- | --- |
| [Create RFQ](/hyperliquid/api-reference/rfqs/create-rfq) and [Cash out position](/hyperliquid/api-reference/positions/cash-out-position) | 30, shared |
| [Accept quote](/hyperliquid/api-reference/quotes/accept-quote) | 30 |
| [Create quote](/hyperliquid/api-reference/quotes/create-quote) | 120 |
| [Cancel quote](/hyperliquid/api-reference/quotes/cancel-quote) | 120 |
| [Confirm quote](/hyperliquid/api-reference/quotes/confirm-quote) | 120 |
| [List quotes](/hyperliquid/api-reference/quotes/list-quotes) | 600 |
| [Get acceptance](/hyperliquid/api-reference/quotes/get-acceptance) | 600 |
| Position and capital reads | 120, shared |
| [List activity](/hyperliquid/api-reference/account/list-activity) | 120 |

Each route also has a total limit across all callers, so `RATE_LIMITED` can arrive below these figures
under heavy load. Too many open RFQs on one account returns `429 RFQ_INTENT_LIMIT`.
